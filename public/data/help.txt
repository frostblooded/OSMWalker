# ℹ Getting started
## What OSM Walker is {about}
OSM Walker is a food delivery rider on the real streets of a real city, taken from OpenStreetMap. He rides about on his bike, taking a random street at each corner, until an order comes in. Then he rides to the restaurant, picks up the food and takes it to the customer's real house number, and is paid there. You spend his money in the 🛒 Shop on upgrades that make him faster and bring orders sooner, so he earns more and more.

He takes every order by himself; your part is choosing what to buy and when. The game runs while it is open on screen. While it is closed, time stops and he waits where he was (see Real time).

Each page in this Help explains one part of the game, with the real numbers the game uses.
## Choosing a city {city_search}
On the first launch you pick where he starts. Start typing the name of a town or city. After {SEARCH_MIN_CHARS} letters and a short pause, suggestions appear by themselves; you can also tap 🔍 or press Enter to search right away. Only towns, cities, villages and suburbs are suggested, and missing accents or small typos are fine ("compiegne" finds Compiegne). Tap a suggestion to start there.

If the search servers can't be reached, it says "Couldn't reach the search server": check your connection and try again. "Nothing found" means there really is no such place. Answers are remembered until you close the game, so searching the same name again is instant.

The game then downloads the streets around that spot. He starts on the street corner nearest to the result.

The area of the result decides the city box Debug shows. It is grown or shrunk so that it reaches at least {MIN_CITY_KM} km and at most {MAX_CITY_KM} km from the centre, so a tiny village still has room to walk and a whole country never counts as one city.
## Real time {offline}
While the game is open on screen he lives in real time: one minute on your clock is one minute for him. A single step never counts for more than {MAX_STEP_S} second of your time, so a stall or a hidden tab can't make him jump ahead.

When you close the game or hide the tab, time stops: he waits where he is and goes on from there when you come back. Nothing is lost while it is closed.

The game saves every {SAVE_EVERY_S} seconds and when you leave, on this device only. Clearing the browser's site data starts you over. If a save can't be read, the game says so and starts over, and keeps a copy of the old save on the device in case it can be fixed.
# 🚲 Riding
## How he rides {walking}
He rides his bike at {BIKE_KMH} km/h, about {BIKE_M_PER_MIN} m a minute, along real streets and paths; motorways are left out. That is on purpose {SPEED_X} times a real city bike's {REAL_BIKE_KMH} km/h door to door, lights and traffic included, so the game plays at a good pace. Each 🔧 Bike tune-up makes him {SPEED_STEP_PCT}% faster (see Upgrades).

With no order he wanders: at each corner he takes a random street, and only turns back at a dead end. With an order he takes the shortest route over the streets loaded so far, and plans again as more load. If the streets ahead of him are not downloaded yet, he stands at the edge and "Loading streets…" shows at the top until they arrive.

If he has ridden more than {TRIP_RATIO} times the straight-line distance to a stop plus {TRIP_EXTRA_KM} km and still isn't there, it is probably across water or behind a fence: he gives up, the order is cancelled without pay, and the feed says so. He counts as arrived when he is within {ARRIVE_M} m of a stop.
## Orders {orders}
An order comes after about {ORDER_WAIT_S} seconds of riding about on average, sometimes sooner, sometimes later (never under {MIN_WAIT_S} seconds). Each 📱 Delivery app boost makes the wait {WAIT_STEP_PCT}% shorter. The wait only runs while he rides, not while streets load.

He takes each order by himself. He picks up at one of the {NEAREST_FOOD} food places nearest him (a {FOOD_KINDS} place; any place of note where there is none) and delivers to a real house number {DROP_MIN_M} m to {DROP_MAX_KM} km from it. Where the map has no house number that far from the restaurant, a street corner stands in for the door. On the map, an orange 1 marks the restaurant and a 2 the door.

The top card shows the order: "🍽 Picking up at …", then "🏠 Delivering to …", with the distance, the riding time and the pay. Tap that line to see him and his stop together; tap it again to go back to him.
## Pay {places}
An order pays {BASE_EURO} plus {PER_KM_EURO} per km from the restaurant to the door, counting the ride along the streets as {ROUTE_FACTOR} times the straight line. So a door {EX_NEAR_M} m from the restaurant pays {EX_NEAR_EURO} and one {EX_FAR_KM} km off pays {EX_FAR_EURO}. The ride to the restaurant isn't paid. The pay is fixed when the order comes, and he gets it at the door, with a 💶 over him for {POSE_S} seconds.
## The map {map}
The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenFreeMap; the ⓘ at the bottom left shows the credits when you tap it. He is the round orange face 🙂, with a bubble over his head showing where he is heading (🍕 a pizza place, 🏠 the customer's door).

While the camera follows him (the Camera follows him switch in the menu, on at first) the map stays still while he rides around the middle, and glides over to him once he is more than {MIDDLE_PCT}% of the view away from the middle. That way the whole map is only redrawn every so often, which saves battery. Drag the map to look around, then tap "Back to him" to follow him again. With the switch off the map never moves by itself.

Use the + and − buttons on the right to zoom in and out; the zoom is remembered. Streets are downloaded in squares around him, {STREET_SQUARES} by {STREET_SQUARES} map tiles of about {TILE_KM} km each (smaller far from the equator), and the squares in the direction he is heading load first. Streets he has seen are kept on the device for {CACHE_DAYS} days, so the same city loads faster next time and works with a weak connection.
# 💶 Progress
## Money {coins}
Money is shown in euros on the top card. He earns it only by delivering orders (see Pay) and spends it in the Shop.
## Upgrades {upgrades}
The 🛒 Shop button at the bottom opens the Shop any time. A dot on it means he can buy something now. Each upgrade can be bought again and again, costs {GROWTH_PCT}% more each time, and works at once:
{UPGRADES}

The 📱 Delivery app boost opens once he has {OPENS_AFTER} Bike tune-ups; until then the Shop shows how many he has.
# 📱 Screens and buttons
## The top card and buttons {screen}
The card at the top shows the city, the street he is on, his money, and his order with the distance, the riding time and the pay (see Orders). What he just did pops up under it for a few seconds.

Only a few buttons sit on the map, at the bottom in reach of your thumb: + and − to zoom and ☰ for the menu on the right, and 🛒 Shop (see Upgrades). "Back to him" shows up at the bottom when the map has moved away from him while the camera follows him.
## The menu {menu}
☰ Menu (bottom right) opens from the bottom of the screen and holds History, Stats, Help, Debug, the Route to goal, Camera follows him and City area switches, and New city. Tap an entry to open it; ✕ Close or the Escape key closes it again.

To see more of the map, drag any sheet down by its title bar. It stays at the height where you let go, up to {SHEET_TALL_PCT}% of the screen (all of it for full-screen sheets like Help). Let go below {SHEET_CLOSE_PCT}% of the screen and it closes.

Route to goal (a switch, off at first and remembered) draws his planned route to his next stop in orange: a solid line along the streets he will take, then a dashed straight line for the last part where the streets are not loaded yet.

Camera follows him (a switch, on at first and remembered) keeps him in view (see The map).
## The Stats sheet {sheet}
🧍 Stats shows his money, the orders he has delivered and how many places he has been to (restaurants and doors). Below it, how many of each upgrade he owns, and what they add up to: his speed and the average wait for an order.
## History {history}
📜 History lists his last {HISTORY_SIZE} events, newest first, each with the time it happened: setting off, each order as it came, each pick-up, each delivery with its pay, cancelled orders and upgrades bought.

While it is open, the map shows the trail he rode (up to about {TRAIL_KM} km), with numbered markers where things happened. Events at the same spot share one marker, such as "4-6". Tap an event to zoom the map to where it happened, then "Back to him" (while the camera follows him) to follow him again.
## Debug {debug}
Debug (in the menu) is for testing and curious players.

At the top, coloured boxes list what looks wrong with the city right now. Cards below show the city (its box, where he is and what he is doing) and what has loaded. 🛎 New order drops the order he is on and brings a new one at once.

Then come two logs, closed at first: Problems (the last {MAX_ERRORS} errors and {MAX_WARNINGS} warnings from anywhere in the game) and Orders (the last {DEBUG_SIZE} orders, deliveries and cancellations). Each entry is a short title, such as "🛎 Order: Pizza Roma → Vitosha 12". Tap an order to see the detail: how far the pick-up and the drop were, the pay, and how many food places and doors were loaded (and whether street corners stood in for doors). A cancelled order counts as a warning. A red dot on ☰ and Debug means there are problems you haven't looked at yet. Problems are kept even through New city.

City area (a switch in the menu) shades the city box in orange and draws the city's real border from OpenStreetMap in purple, so you can see where the two differ. The switch is remembered.
## New city {new_city}
New city (in the menu) starts the whole game over in a city you choose. Because it wipes everything (money, upgrades, deliveries, places visited, History, the order and its log), you have to type RESET in capitals and confirm.
## About Help {help}
Help (in the menu) explains every part of the game on its own page. Tap a topic to open it and Back to return to the list.

To find something fast, type in the search box at the top. Results appear as you type: every page that holds all the words you typed, in any order, with pages whose title matches first. Words are matched from their start ("fast" finds "faster", but "fee" doesn't find "coffee"), and capitals and accents don't matter ("cafe" finds "Café"). Each result shows the line where your word was found, with the word marked. Tap one to open the page; Back brings you to your results, and ✖ clears the box and shows the full list again.

The numbers on these pages come straight from the game, so they are always the ones it really uses. Help is updated whenever a mechanic changes.
