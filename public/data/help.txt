# ℹ Getting started
## What OSM Walker is {about}
OSM Walker is a little character you watch walk the real streets of a real city, taken from OpenStreetMap. He walks to a place, gets what it gives him, and heads for the next one. Work places pay him money; sights and green spaces give him XP, and enough XP takes him up a level.

The game runs while it is open on screen. While it is closed, time stops and he waits where he was (see Real time).

Each page in this Help explains one part of the game, with the real numbers the game uses.
## Choosing a city {city_search}
On the first launch you pick where he starts. Start typing the name of a town or city. After {SEARCH_MIN_CHARS} letters and a short pause, suggestions appear by themselves; you can also tap 🔍 or press Enter to search right away. Only towns, cities, villages and suburbs are suggested, and missing accents or small typos are fine ("compiegne" finds Compiegne). Tap a suggestion to start there.

If the search servers can't be reached, it says "Couldn't reach the search server": check your connection and try again. "Nothing found" means there really is no such place. Answers are remembered until you close the game, so searching the same name again is instant.

The game then downloads the streets around that spot. He starts on the street corner nearest to the result and stays inside that city.

The area of the result decides the city box he picks goals in. It is grown or shrunk so that it reaches at least {MIN_CITY_KM} km and at most {MAX_CITY_KM} km from the centre, so a tiny village still has room to walk and a whole country never counts as one city.
## Real time {offline}
While the game is open on screen he lives in real time: one minute on your clock is one minute for him. A single step never counts for more than {MAX_STEP_S} second of your time, so a stall or a hidden tab can't make him jump ahead.

When you close the game or hide the tab, time stops: he waits where he is and goes on from there when you come back. Nothing is lost while it is closed.

The game saves every {SAVE_EVERY_S} seconds and when you leave, on this device only. Clearing the browser's site data starts you over. If a save can't be read, the game says so and starts over, and keeps a copy of the old save on the device in case it can be fixed.
# 🚶 Walking
## How he walks {walking}
He walks at {WALK_KMH} km/h, about {WALK_M_PER_MIN} m a minute, along real streets, paths and footways. Motorways and other roads you can't walk on are left out.

He always walks towards a goal (see Places and rewards and His goal). He plans the shortest route over the streets that are loaded so far, and plans again as more streets load. If the streets ahead of him are not downloaded yet, he stands at the edge and "Loading streets…" shows at the top until they arrive.

If he has walked more than {TRIP_RATIO} times the straight-line distance to his goal plus {TRIP_EXTRA_KM} km and still isn't there, the goal is probably across water or behind a fence, so he gives up on it and picks another. After more than {MAX_SKIPS} give-ups in a row he just walks down a random street for a while.
## Places and rewards {places}
His goals are named places from the map. Only places that give him something count, and each kind gives one thing:
- 💶 work places pay {WORK_EURO} a visit: {WORK_LIST},
- ⭐ sights give {SIGHT_XP} XP: {SIGHT_LIST},
- 🌳 green spaces give {GREEN_XP} XP: {GREEN_LIST}.

The first time he reaches a place he gets {NEW_PLACE_XP} XP on top. A place is remembered by its name and where it is. He counts as arrived when he is within {ARRIVE_M} m of it, and what he got shows in the feed under the top card and as a 💶 or ⭐ over him for {POSE_S} seconds.

Only places inside the city box count, and places closer than {ARRIVE_M} m to him are skipped so he always really walks somewhere. If none have loaded yet, he heads for a random spot in the city box instead and gets nothing there.
## His goal {goal}
For now he picks his next goal himself. Every rewarding place in the city box gets a weight, and the bigger its weight the likelier it is picked. The weight is three numbers multiplied together:

Rank: 1 / rank, where rank 1 is the most important place in its area, so a rank 4 place weighs 0.25.

Distance: places about {GOAL_PEAK_M} m away weigh most (1). The weight falls off smoothly both ways: a place {NEAR_M} m away weighs {NEAR_PCT} as much, one {FAR_KM} km away {FAR_PCT}.

Heading: a place straight ahead along his last walk weighs {HEADING_RATIO} times one straight behind him, with places to the side in between. Right after starting he has no heading yet, so every direction counts the same.

Debug shows all three numbers for every place picked. Random spots follow the same distance and heading odds, and always stay inside the city box.

While he walks, the card at the top shows where he is going, how far it is and how long the walk will take, for example "🎯 Going to Central Market · 1.2 km · ~14 min". The time is the planned route at his speed. A 🎯 pin on the map marks the goal. Tap that line to see him and his goal on the map together; tap it again to go back to him.
## The map {map}
The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenFreeMap; the ⓘ at the bottom left shows the credits when you tap it. He is the round orange face 🙂, with a bubble over his head showing the kind of place he is heading for (☕ a cafe, 🏛 a museum).

While the camera follows him (the Camera follows him switch in the menu, on at first) the map stays still while he walks around the middle, and glides over to him once he is more than {MIDDLE_PCT}% of the view away from the middle. That way the whole map is only redrawn every so often, which saves battery. Drag the map to look around, then tap "Back to him" to follow him again. With the switch off the map never moves by itself.

Use the + and − buttons on the right to zoom in and out; the zoom is remembered. Streets are downloaded in squares around him, {STREET_SQUARES} by {STREET_SQUARES} map tiles of about {TILE_KM} km each (smaller far from the equator), and the squares in the direction he is heading load first. Streets he has seen are kept on the device for {CACHE_DAYS} days, so the same city loads faster next time and works with a weak connection.
# ⭐ Progress
## Money {coins}
Money is shown in euros on the top card. He earns {WORK_EURO} at each work place he reaches (see Places and rewards). For now money only adds up; spending it comes later.
## XP and levels {xp}
He gains XP from sights and green spaces, and {NEW_PLACE_XP} XP the first time he reaches any place (see Places and rewards).

Each level needs {XP_PER_LEVEL} x his level in XP: {XP_LV1} XP from level 1 to 2, {XP_LV2} XP from 2 to 3, {XP_LV10} XP from 10 to 11. Extra XP carries over to the next level, and each level-up shows in the feed.
# 📱 Screens and buttons
## The top card and buttons {screen}
The card at the top shows the city, the street he is on, his money and level, and his goal with the distance and the walking time. What he just did pops up under it for a few seconds.

Only three buttons sit on the map, at the bottom right in reach of your thumb: + and − to zoom, and ☰ for the menu. "Back to him" shows up at the bottom when the map has moved away from him while the camera follows him.
## The menu {menu}
☰ Menu (bottom right) opens from the bottom of the screen and holds History, Stats, Help, Debug, the Route to goal, Camera follows him and City area switches, and New city. Tap an entry to open it; ✕ Close or the Escape key closes it again.

To see more of the map, drag any sheet down by its title bar. It stays at the height where you let go, up to {SHEET_TALL_PCT}% of the screen (all of it for full-screen sheets like Help). Let go below {SHEET_CLOSE_PCT}% of the screen and it closes.

Route to goal (a switch, off at first and remembered) draws his planned route in orange: a solid line along the streets he will take, then a dashed straight line for the last part where the streets are not loaded yet.

Camera follows him (a switch, on at first and remembered) keeps him in view (see The map).
## The Stats sheet {sheet}
🧍 Stats shows his level with a bar for the XP to the next one, his money, and how many places he has been to.
## History {history}
📜 History lists his last {HISTORY_SIZE} events, newest first, each with the time it happened: setting off, the places he reached with what he got, and level-ups.

While it is open, the map shows the trail he walked (up to about {TRAIL_KM} km), with numbered markers where things happened. Events at the same spot share one marker, such as "4-6". Tap an event to zoom the map to where it happened, then "Back to him" (while the camera follows him) to follow him again.
## Debug {debug}
Debug (in the menu) is for testing and curious players.

At the top, coloured boxes list what looks wrong with the city right now. Cards below show the city (its box, where he is and what he is doing) and what has loaded. 🎯 New goal makes him pick a new goal now.

Then come two logs, closed at first: Problems (the last {MAX_ERRORS} errors and {MAX_WARNINGS} warnings from anywhere in the game) and Goal picks (his last {DEBUG_SIZE} goals). Each entry is a short title, such as "🎯 Chosen: Cafe East". Tap it to see how it happened: the odds and why. A random spot counts as a warning. A red dot on ☰ and Debug means there are problems you haven't looked at yet. Problems are kept even through New city.

City area (a switch in the menu) shades the box his goals are picked from in orange and draws the city's real border from OpenStreetMap in purple, so you can see where the two differ. The switch is remembered.
## New city {new_city}
New city (in the menu) starts the whole game over in a city you choose. Because it wipes everything (money, level, places visited, History and the goal-pick log), you have to type RESET in capitals and confirm.
## About Help {help}
Help (in the menu) explains every part of the game on its own page. Tap a topic to open it and Back to return to the list.

To find something fast, type in the search box at the top. Results appear as you type: every page that holds all the words you typed, in any order, with pages whose title matches first. Words are matched from their start ("fast" finds "faster", but "fee" doesn't find "coffee"), and capitals and accents don't matter ("cafe" finds "Café"). Each result shows the line where your word was found, with the word marked. Tap one to open the page; Back brings you to your results, and ✖ clears the box and shows the full list again.

The numbers on these pages come straight from the game, so they are always the ones it really uses. Help is updated whenever a mechanic changes.
