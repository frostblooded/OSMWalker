# ℹ Getting started
## What OSM Walker is {about}
OSM Walker is a little life you watch, not steer. A small character walks the real streets of a real city, taken from OpenStreetMap. He picks places to visit, walks there, does something at each one, and slowly gets stronger, richer and more experienced.

You don't control him. Everything he does is decided by the rules on these pages: where he goes, what he does there, what he buys, and when he travels to another city or country. Your part is to check in, see where he has got to, and read what he has been up to. Now and then you can also do something small for him, such as sending a care package (see Little touches). It is always optional: he lives just as well if you never do. He lives on while the game is closed too (see While you were away).

Each page in this Help explains one part of the game in detail, with the real numbers the game uses.
## Choosing a city {city_search}
On the first launch you pick where he starts. Start typing the name of a town or city. After {SEARCH_MIN_CHARS} letters and a short pause, suggestions appear by themselves; you can also tap 🔍 or press Enter to search right away. Only towns, cities, villages and suburbs are suggested, and missing accents or small typos are fine ("compiegne" finds Compiegne). Tap a suggestion to start there.

If the search servers can't be reached, it says "Couldn't reach the search server": check your connection and try again. "Nothing found" means there really is no such place. Answers are remembered until you close the game, so searching the same name again is instant.

The game then downloads the streets around that spot. He starts on the street corner nearest to the result and stays inside that city until he travels somewhere else (see Trips to other cities).

The area of the result decides the city box he picks goals in. It is grown or shrunk so that it reaches at least {MIN_CITY_KM} km and at most {MAX_CITY_KM} km from the centre, so a tiny village still has room to walk and a whole country never counts as one city.
## Real time, and time away {offline}
While the game is open on screen he lives in real time: one minute on your clock is one minute for him. A single step never counts for more than {MAX_STEP_S} second, so a stall can't make him jump ahead.

When you close the game or hide the tab, the time doesn't stop for him. When you come back, the time you were away passes for him in one go, and a "While you were away" card tells you what he did (see While you were away).

The game saves every {SAVE_EVERY_S} seconds and when you leave, on this device only. Clearing the browser's site data starts you over. If a save can't be read, the game says so and starts over, and keeps a copy of the old save on the device in case it can be fixed.
## While you were away {away}
When the game opens again after at least {MIN_AWAY_MIN} minute away (closed, or the tab hidden), that time passes for him at once, at full speed, the same as if you had watched. Only the first {MAX_AWAY_H} hours after you left count, so he lives through at most {MAX_AWAY_H} hours of a longer absence; the card says how much was left over.

He doesn't walk the map while you are away, so no streets have to load. Instead he hops from place to place by the usual rules:
- he picks each place like any goal (see Picking the next goal), from the places already loaded in his city, and walks the straight line to it times {ROAD_FACTOR}, at his usual speed,
- he does one action there and stays {STAY_MIN} minute (see What he does at places),
- his meters, buffs and money change as they always do, so he can get hungry, earn, buy gear, level up, and faint and wake in hospital,
- he rolls the trip dice as usual and pays for the trip (see Trips to other cities). A trip that wouldn't be over before you come back waits until you are there to watch.

In a city whose streets haven't loaded (one he reached while you were away), he visits made-up places of the usual kinds, such as "a cafe in Plovdiv". They have the actions of real places of that kind, but give no first-visit XP, cards, souvenirs, Placedex entries, crawl progress or crawl perks. He leaves such a city from its bus station or a gas station in the middle.

If he was on a bus, a plane or driving his car when you left, the ride finishes first. On reopen the game waits up to {CATCHUP_WAIT_S} seconds for the streets around him to come out of the device's cache, so he visits the real places nearby.

At the end he stands at the last place he visited, on the nearest street corner within {SNAP_M} m, or there on his own until the streets around it load, and picks his next goal. Everything he did is in his History at the time it happened, with a marker for each place but no trail, because he didn't really walk the streets in between.

The card shows how long you were away, how many places he visited and how many were new, how far he walked, the cities he reached, his money and level before and after, and how many things he bought. If the reopen earned a clover (see Clovers and care packages), it says so, and now and then it holds one small choice (see One choice on reopen). Tap "events, newest first" to see everything he did. "Carry on" or ✕ Close closes it.
## His week {recap}
The game keeps a tally of his week: how far he walked, how many places he stopped at and how many were new, the kind of place he stopped at most, the cities he arrived in, and his money and level when the week began. A week starts on Monday at midnight on your phone's clock.

After a week ends, the next While you were away sheet opens with two cards about it, His week and His week's progress, ready for a screenshot. Each week's recap shows once; a week with no stops has none.
## Daily forecast {forecast}
Every day has one forecast, shown on the top card under his goal. It is picked from the date on your phone, so everyone playing on the same date gets the same one, and it lasts until midnight. He doesn't plan around it, but it changes how some things go that day, and so what he can afford:
{FORECAST_LIST}

While you are away, each day he lives through has its own forecast.
# 🚶 Walking
## How he walks {walking}
On foot he walks at {WALK_KMH} km/h, about {WALK_M_PER_MIN} m a minute, along real streets, paths and footways. Motorways and other roads you can't walk on are left out. A bike or a motor vehicle makes him faster (see Speed and vehicles).

He always walks towards a goal (see Places of note and His goal). He plans the shortest route over the streets that are loaded so far, and plans again as more streets load. If the streets ahead of him are not downloaded yet, he stands at the edge and "Loading streets…" shows at the top until they arrive.

If he has walked more than 2.5 times the straight-line distance to his goal plus 2 km and still isn't there, the goal is probably across water or behind a fence, so he gives up on it and picks another. After {MAX_SKIPS} give-ups in a row he just walks down a random street for a while.
## Speed and vehicles {speed}
His speed depends on how he gets about:
- on foot: {WALK_KMH} km/h,
- bike: {BIKE_KMH} km/h,
- scooter or motorbike: {MOTORCYCLE_KMH} km/h,
- car: {CITY_CAR_KMH} km/h (city traffic),
- a fantasy mount: see Fantasy mounts.

He rides whatever he wears in his vehicle slot. Bike shops sell bikes, motorcycle dealers scooters and motorbikes, and car dealers cars. A car always beats a motorbike, and a motorbike a bike, whatever their material. A vehicle's material adds {SPEED_PER_POWER}% speed per point of power, and its modifiers don't add speed.

Every level above 1 adds {SPEED_PER_LEVEL_PCT} speed. When his energy is under {TIRED_ENERGY} he slows down, to half speed at 0 energy.

Riding is less tiring than walking: a bike takes {BIKE_EFFORT_PCT} of the energy per km, and a motorbike or car {MOTOR_EFFORT_PCT}. The goal line's time ("12 min") uses his speed right now.
## Fantasy mounts {mounts}
Now and then the real map has a little magic in it. These mounts can be had, each from a real place of the right kind:
- a giant tortoise from a pet shop: {TORTOISE_KMH} km/h, and he never tires on it,
- a broomstick from a hardware or DIY shop: {BROOM_KMH} km/h,
- a flying carpet from a carpet shop: {CARPET_KMH} km/h,
- a dragon from the stable at a real castle: {DRAGON_KMH} km/h.

The tortoise, broomstick and carpet sit beside the bike, scooter and car: each ranks with that vehicle, so he swaps one for the other only when the new one is stronger (a better material). A pet, hardware or carpet shop only has one in {MOUNT_STOCK_PCT} of the time he asks. The dragon is the top tier, above any car: from {CHEAP_DRAGON}, it is what he saves up for once he has a car (see Saving for a vehicle).

Flyers (the broomstick, the carpet and the dragon) don't follow the streets. When his goal is at least {FLY_MIN_M} m away and a park or garden is loaded near it (at most {LAND_MAX_M} m from the goal, and at most {LAND_SHARE_PCT} as far from it as he is), he flies there in a straight line, lands, and walks the rest. Otherwise he goes by the streets like anyone else. While you're away the same rules decide when he flies. On the map his mount shows at his side.

A dragon eats {DRAGON_UPKEEP} of gold a day. He pays it first thing each day, before he moves on (also while you're away); if he hasn't got it, the dragon sulks and won't fly (if it starts to sulk in the air, he comes down at the nearest corner), and he walks until he can pay. Nothing is lost. On a day it is fed it brings back treasure {DRAGON_TREASURE_PCT} of the time, worth {DRAGON_TREASURE_MIN} to {DRAGON_TREASURE_MAX}. Riding a broomstick takes {BROOM_EFFORT_PCT} of the walking energy per km, a carpet {CARPET_EFFORT_PCT} and a dragon {DRAGON_EFFORT_PCT}.
## Places of note {places}
His goals are named places from the map: shops, cafes, restaurants, parks, museums, churches, stations and many more. Only places inside the current city box count, and places closer than {ARRIVE_M} m to him are skipped so he always really walks somewhere.

Each place has a rank from the map, where 1 is the most important in its area, so big sights and busy shops come up more often than small ones. How far away a place is and which way it lies count too (see Picking the next goal).

If no places have loaded yet, he heads for a random spot in the city box instead and walks on through it without stopping.

He counts as arrived when he is within {ARRIVE_M} m of the place.
## Made-up places {made_places}
The real map comes first, but a city sometimes lacks what he needs, and quests need somewhere to happen. So once the streets around him have loaded in a new city, the game adds a few places of its own. They show on the map with the game's own round icons (see Map icons) and work like any other place: he picks them as goals and does their actions. They give no cards, souvenirs, Placedex entries or crawl progress, since those are for real places, but the first of each extra kind and his first wizard's tower count as odd places in the Collection.

Must-haves fill gaps. If none of the loaded places is one of these, the city gets one near its centre, on a street:
- a bus station ("Plovdiv coach station"), so he can leave,
- a gas station, so a car can refuel,
- somewhere to eat (an action that fills him by at least {MEAL_FULLNESS}),
- somewhere to sleep (an action that gives at least {BED_ENERGY} energy),
- a job centre, so he can always earn.

A city of {AIRPORT_POP} people or more with no real airport within {AIRPORT_KM} km gets an airport of its own outside it, so planes can fly there too.

Extras give stories a stage: notice boards, an adventurers' hall, old wells, lookouts, hidden shrines and storytellers' corners, each next to a fitting real spot (a well in a park, a lookout at a viewpoint), or near the centre when the city has no such spot. How many a city gets depends on its size:
- a town (up to {TOWN_POP} people): {EXTRAS_TOWN},
- a city (up to {CITY_POP} people): {EXTRAS_CITY} and {TOWERS_CITY} wizard's tower,
- a big city: {EXTRAS_CAPITAL} and {TOWERS_CAPITAL} wizard's towers ({TOWERS_MEGA} above {MEGA_POP} people).

Wizard's towers rise at the grandest real landmarks (castles first, then viewpoints, sights, monuments and museums), at least {TOWER_APART_KM} km apart. A city with no landmark gets none.

A city keeps its made-up places: they are rolled from the city's name and saved, so the tower is still at the same castle when he comes back. The save keeps them for the {MADE_CITIES} cities he was in most recently.
## Map icons {map_icons}
The game marks its own places on the map with round icons: blue for travel (bus stations, airports, gas stations), green for food and beds, amber for work, purple for story places and indigo for magic. Real bus stations and the airports he can fly from get one too. Icons show once a whole city fits in view, and their names show from street level. At most the {ICON_CAP} nearest to him show at once, and none while he rides to another city.
## Picking the next goal {goal_pick}
He picks his next goal like someone with a plan for the day: not always the nearest place, not across town on a whim, and mostly carrying on the way he was going. Every place in the city box gets a weight, and the bigger its weight the likelier it is picked. The weight is four numbers multiplied together:

Rank: 1 / rank, so a rank 1 place weighs 1 and a rank 4 place weighs 0.25.

Distance: places about {GOAL_PEAK_M} m away weigh most (1). The weight falls off smoothly both ways: a place {NEAR_M} m away weighs {NEAR_PCT} as much, one {FAR_KM} km away {FAR_PCT}, so he hops between nearby spots and only now and then crosses the city.

Heading: his heading is the direction from where his last walk started to where it ended. A place straight ahead weighs {HEADING_RATIO} times one straight behind him, with places to the side in between. Right after arriving in a new city he has no heading yet, so every direction counts the same.

Need: a place weighs 1 + {NEED_BOOST} x the need score of the best thing he can do there right now, times how well it suits him (see His needs). So a place that meets an empty need in full weighs {NEED_MAX} times as much, and at half of {NEED_AT} {NEED_HALF} times. When he is hungry he heads for food, when tired for coffee or a bed, and when he is broke or saving for a trip or a vehicle, for paid work. A full bag counts as an empty need met in full by resale shops (see The bag and selling). Once he has saved up for his next vehicle and isn't saving for a fare, a shop that sells it weighs {GOAL_SHOP_PULL} times as much (see Saving for a vehicle). Only an urgent need beats that. A whisper he follows (see Whispers) changes this too: Eat and Rest make those meters feel empty, so they count as rock bottom (see Urgent below), Save counts as saving for a trip, and Explore makes each place he has never been to weigh {EXPLORE_PULL} times as much.

Urgent: while a meter is under {URGENT_AT} and some place in the city box can fix it, he only looks at places that fix an urgent need, and rank, the {GOAL_PEAK_M} m bump and heading drop out: the nearest fix wins. A fix {URGENT_NEAR_M} m away or closer weighs most, one twice as far a quarter as much. He still does as he likes {SLIP_PCT} of the time, unless a meter is under {BOTTOM_AT}: then he always goes, and only places that fix that meter count, even over another urgent need.

Random spots (while no places are loaded) follow the same distance and heading odds, and always stay inside the city box. Debug shows all four numbers for every pick.
## His goal {goal}
While he walks, the card at the top shows where he is going, how far it is and how long the walk will take, for example "🎯 Going to Central Market · 1.2 km · ~14 min". The time is approximate: it is the planned route at his current speed. A 🎯 pin on the map marks the goal.

Tap that line to see him and his goal on the map together. Tap the line again (or "Back to him" while the camera follows him) to go back to him. The view also goes back to him by itself when he picks a new goal.

While he stays at a place the line reads "Resting at" and the place's name.
## Staying somewhere {countdown}
At each place of note he stops and does one action (see What he does at places). Every stop lasts exactly {STAY_MIN} minute, whatever the action is, so he is never standing still for long.

While he stays, the top card shows "Moving again in 0:59" and an orange bar that fills up as the minute passes. When it is full he picks his next goal and sets off.
## The map {map}
The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenFreeMap; the ⓘ at the bottom left shows the credits when you tap it. He is the round orange face (see His face and bubble). The game moves him a few times a second and his face glides smoothly between those steps.

While the camera follows him (the Camera follows him switch in the menu, on at first) the map stays still while he walks around the middle, and glides over to him once he is more than {MIDDLE_PCT}% of the view away from the middle. That way the whole map is only redrawn every so often, which saves battery. Drag the map to look around, then tap "Back to him" to follow him again. With the switch off the map never moves by itself, and there is no "Back to him".

Use the + and − buttons on the right to zoom in and out; the zoom is remembered. Streets are downloaded in squares around him, {STREET_SQUARES} by {STREET_SQUARES} map tiles of about {TILE_KM} km each (smaller far from the equator), and the squares in the direction he is heading load first. Streets he has seen are kept on the device for {CACHE_DAYS} days, so the same city loads faster next time and works with a weak connection.

During a trip to another city a red line shows the way from where he left to where he is going, and the map zooms out to fit it.
## His face and bubble {look}
His face shows how he feels. The first of these that fits wins:
- 🤒 sick: health below {SICK_AT},
- 😩 tired: energy below {TIRED_ENERGY},
- 😖 dirty: hygiene below {DIRTY_AT},
- 😞 down: mood below {LOW_MOOD},
- 😄 happy: mood {HAPPY_MOOD} or more,
- 🙂 fine: anything else.

While he walks, a bubble over his head shows what he needs most: the icon of his lowest meter under {NEED_AT} (🍔 food, 🥤 a drink, 🛏 rest, 🚿 a wash, 💊 health, 🎈 fun). When he needs nothing it shows the kind of place he is going to (☕ a cafe, 🏛 a museum). On the way to a random spot it shows only a need, and on a trip there is no bubble.

While he stays at a place, a pose for what he is doing floats over him instead: 💤 a nap, ☕ a coffee, 🎵 something fun, 💡 learning, 💶 work, 🛍 shopping, and so on, after what the action gives most. It moves for a few seconds when he gets there, then holds still to save battery.
## City sound {sound}
The 🔊 City sound switch in the menu (off at first) plays a soft loop for where he is: cafe chatter with the odd clinking cup while he stays at a place to eat or drink, birds and leaves in parks and gardens, and a low street hum anywhere else. On a trip it is quiet. The sound is made on your phone, so it downloads nothing; it stops while the game is in the background.
# 🎯 Actions
## What he does at places {actions}
Every kind of place has its own list of actions. At a cafe he might have an espresso or chat with the barista, at a park he might nap under a tree, at a shop he might browse for gear or sell his loot. There are {ACTION_COUNT} actions for {PLACE_KINDS} kinds of places, plus a few general ones for places that don't have their own list yet.

An action can:
- raise or lower his meters (health, energy, fullness, thirst, mood, hygiene),
- raise his attributes (strength, charm, wits, luck), usually by a small step such as 0.1,
- give XP,
- cost money or pay money (jobs pay a fixed amount or a random amount in a range, to the cent, and more as he grows: see Wages),
- give him a buff or debuff for a while (see Buffs and debuffs),
- offer him an item to buy, or sell something from his bag.

Some actions have a risk: a chance that it goes differently, which adds to what it says, for example "Rakia with the salad, one too many". A risk can cost extra money and change his meters, for better or worse.

What he did pops up at the top for a few seconds, and goes into his History. About {JOKE_PCT} of these lines (not buying or selling) get a dry joke on the end, picked for the kind of place: "Looked at the paintings at the National Museum. The museum did not visit back."
## How he chooses {choosing}
At each place he first throws out every action he can't do right now (see Requirements). From the rest he picks one at random, but some actions weigh more than others:
- Every action starts with weight 1.
- Browsing for an item: +1 when he has more than {SKINT_X3}.
- Selling his loot: +2.
- Anything that would leave him under {SKINT} weighs half as much.
- A country's own actions get +{FLAVOUR_WEIGHT} (see Country flavour).
- While he saves up for a trip or his next vehicle, spending weighs {SAVING_SPEND_PCT} as much (see Fares and Saving for a vehicle).
- Browsing the shop of the vehicle he has saved up for: +{GOAL_SHOP}.
- A kind deed while he leans kind: +{KIND_DEED_WEIGHT} (see Kind or rascal).

His needs here are the ones he feels: while he follows an Eat or Rest whisper, the meters it names count as empty (see Whispers).

Then his needs take over (see His needs): the weight is multiplied by 1 + {NEED_BOOST} x the action's need score, by how well it suits him, and cut for every need it drains. With a meter under {URGENT_AT}, he only picks from actions that fix an urgent need, but for {SLIP_PCT} of the time; under {BOTTOM_AT}, always, and only from actions that fix that meter.

An action with weight 4 is four times as likely as one with weight 1.
## His needs {urges}
His needs come first, like real ones: a little hungry he may still visit a museum, starving he goes straight for food.

Urge: each meter (fullness, thirst, energy, health, mood, hygiene) is a need below {NEED_AT}. Its urge is the square of how far below it is: 0 at {NEED_AT}, {URGE_HALF} at {NEED_HALF_AT}, 1 at 0. So it grows slowly at first and steeply near empty. Money is a need too: below {SKINT} its urge grows the same way, down to 1 at €0, and while he saves up for a trip or a vehicle it is at least {SAVING_URGE}.

Need score: an action scores the urge of each meter it raises, times how much of the need it meets: a raise of {FULL_FIX} or more meets it in full, a smaller one in part. Paid work scores the money urge, and with a full bag a resale shop's sale scores 1. The scores of all needs an action meets add up, so a meal with a drink beats a meal alone when he is both hungry and thirsty.

Urgent: under {URGENT_AT} a meter is urgent. Under {BOTTOM_AT} it is at rock bottom, and fixing it beats every other need. While any meter is urgent he skips the trip dice and stays in town. A meter an Eat or Rest whisper names feels empty while he follows it, so it counts as rock bottom (see Whispers). See Picking the next goal and How he chooses.

The feed says when a strong need picks his goal, for example "Hungry, heading for Bakery Nord": an urgent need, or one at half of {NEED_AT} or lower. Money only counts there while he has less than {SKINT}, not while he just saves up.

Drain: an action that lowers a meter he needs weighs (1 - that meter's urge) as much, at least {HARM_FLOOR_PCT}, so he doesn't go jogging when he is worn out.

His strengths: an action that trains or needs one of his attributes weighs (that attribute / his average attribute) as much, from 1 up to {STRENGTH_MAX} times, so a strong walker leans to the gym and a charming one to people. A place weighs as much as its best-suited action.
## Requirements {needs}
An action can only be picked when he meets all of its requirements:
- Money: he needs at least the action's price.
- Shopping for an item needs more than {KEEP_CENTS}.
- Selling needs something in his bag.
- Attributes: some need a minimum strength, charm, wits or luck, counting his gear. For example arm-wrestling needs strength 4 and volunteering as a museum guide needs wits 6.
- Level: some need a minimum level.
- Time of day, by your device's clock: morning {TIME_MORNING}, day {TIME_DAY}, evening {TIME_EVENING}, night {TIME_NIGHT}. Bars serve rakia only in the evening, for example.
- Low meters: some only happen when a meter is low, such as napping under a tree when his energy is under 40, or the soup kitchen when his fullness is under 30.
## Money {coins}
His money is in euros, counted to the cent and shown like {EXAMPLE_PRICE}. He starts with {START_CENTS}. Money never goes below €0.

Prices are real-world euro prices, the same in every country: a coffee is a few euros, a hostel bed about €28, a bike from {CHEAP_BIKE} and a car from {CHEAP_CAR}. Wages start from real ones too, but grow as he levels up (see Wages).

He spends money on food, drinks, tickets, trips and gear, and earns it from jobs, from selling items and from lucky finds. While he has less than {SKINT} money is a need, steeper the closer he gets to €0, and he leans away from spending (see His needs).

He keeps a small reserve: he won't buy gear that would leave him with less than {KEEP_CENTS}. Until he has a dragon he saves up for his next vehicle (see Saving for a vehicle). Waking up in hospital costs {HOSPITAL_FEE}, but never more than {HOSPITAL_MAX_PCT} of his money.
## Wages {wages}
Every job lists a real-world wage for a shift, such as a café paying for washing cups. At level 1 a job with no attribute need pays exactly that, unless the daily forecast raises pay for the day (see Daily forecast). As he grows he earns more, well above real wages, so that vehicles come within reach:
- {WAGE_LEVEL_PCT} more for every level above 1,
- {WAGE_ATTR_PCT} more for every point of the attribute the job needs, counting his gear. Tending the bar needs charm, so with charm 10 it pays {WAGE_ATTR_X10} more.

For example a job with no attribute need pays {WAGE_LV11} its listed wage at level 11, and {WAGE_LV30} at level 30. Only pay grows: prices stay the same at every level. Anything that pays counts, including bets, gigs and lucky finds at places such as the coin by the ATM. Selling items and money found while walking don't grow.
## Saving for a vehicle {saving}
He always saves up for the cheapest vehicle one step above what he rides:
- on foot, a {GOAL_BIKE} for {CHEAP_BIKE},
- on a bike, a {GOAL_SCOOTER} for {CHEAP_SCOOTER},
- on a scooter or motorbike, a {GOAL_CAR} for {CHEAP_CAR},
- in a car, a {GOAL_DRAGON} for {CHEAP_DRAGON} (see Fantasy mounts).

A fantasy mount counts as the vehicle it sits beside: on a tortoise he saves for a scooter, on a broomstick for a car, on a carpet for a dragon. With a dragon there is nothing more to save for. He saves up to the price plus his reserve of {KEEP_CENTS}. Until he has it, money is a need of at least {SAVING_URGE} urge and spending weighs {SAVING_SPEND_PCT} as much (see His needs), so paid work and places that pay pull him.

Once he has the money (and isn't still saving for a trip fare), a shop that sells that vehicle weighs {GOAL_SHOP_PULL} times as much as an ordinary place when he picks his next goal, so he heads for one, and browsing there gets +{GOAL_SHOP}. A bike shop or vehicle dealer shows him {DEALER_OFFERS} random vehicles and he buys the best one he can afford while keeping his reserve. If all of them cost too much (a better material or modifiers raise the price), he walks on and tries again later. The Stats sheet shows his goal under Saving for.
## Buffs and debuffs {buffs}
Some actions leave an effect that lasts a while, shown with the minutes left on the Stats sheet. Buffs count down in game minutes, so they only run down while the game is open.

A buff can change a meter every hour it lasts (Caffeinated: +5 energy per hour; Healthy: +1 health per hour; Hungover: -3 energy and -2 mood per hour), or multiply the XP he gets from actions (Inspired: double XP). Several XP buffs multiply together. A buff can also raise an attribute while it lasts: Lucky (from a big tip or rubbing a statue) adds {LUCKY_LUCK} luck.

Doing an action that gives a buff he already has starts that buff over at its full length instead of stacking it.

# ❤ Body stats
## Health {health}
Health (HP, the red bar) goes from 0 to 100 and starts at {START_HEALTH}.

He loses health when he is starving or parched: {STARVING_PER_H} health per hour while fullness is at 0, and another {STARVING_PER_H} per hour while thirst is at 0. Some risky actions and drinks also cost a little health.

He gets health back from hospitals, doctors, pharmacies, rest and good food, and from buffs such as Healthy.

When health reaches 0 he faints and wakes up in hospital (see Fainting and hospital).
## Energy {energy}
Energy (EN, the yellow bar) goes from 0 to 100 and starts at {START_ENERGY}.

He uses {ENERGY_PER_KM} energy for every km he walks, less on a bike or in a motor vehicle (see Speed and vehicles), and less on a tailwind day (see Daily forecast). Coffee, food, rest, naps and buffs such as Caffeinated give it back; hard jobs, workouts and debuffs such as Hungover or Crash take it away.

When energy is under {NEED_AT} he needs rest more and more (see His needs), and shuns actions that tire him. Napping under a tree only happens when it is under {NAP_BELOW}. Under {TIRED_ENERGY} he slows down, to half speed at 0, but he never stops. Nothing else happens at 0 energy.
## Fullness {fullness}
Fullness (FU, the green bar) is how fed he is, from 0 to 100, starting at {START_FULLNESS}.

It drops by {FULLNESS_PER_H} every hour the game is open, so from full it lasts about {FULLNESS_HOURS} hours. Meals, snacks and buffs such as Snacking fill it.

Under {NEED_AT} he needs food more and more (see His needs); under {URGENT_AT} he heads straight for it. At 0 he starts losing {STARVING_PER_H} health per hour until he eats.
## Thirst {thirst}
Thirst is how well watered he is, from 0 to 100 (higher is better), starting at {START_THIRST}.

It drops by {THIRST_PER_H} every hour the game is open, so from full it lasts about {THIRST_HOURS} hours. Drinks, drinking fountains, cafes and bars fill it.

Under {NEED_AT} he needs a drink more and more (see His needs); under {URGENT_AT} he heads straight for one. At 0 he starts losing {STARVING_PER_H} health per hour until he drinks.
## Mood {mood}
Mood is how happy he is, from 0 to 100, starting at {START_MOOD}. The daily forecast can make one kind of place lift it more for a day (see Daily forecast).

It drops by {MOOD_PER_H} every hour the game is open, and by {DIRTY_MOOD_PER_H} more while he is dirty (see Hygiene). Treats, sights, music, chats and buffs such as Tipsy or Blessed raise it; failed bets, risks gone wrong and debuffs such as Hungover lower it.

When mood is under {NEED_AT} he needs cheering up more and more (see His needs).

Mood scales the XP he gets from actions: ×{MOOD_XP_MIN} at mood 0, ×1 at 50 and ×{MOOD_XP_MAX} at 100, in a straight line between. For example at mood 80 an action worth 20 XP gives {MOOD_80_XP}. The Stats sheet shows the multiplier now. First-visit XP is not scaled.
## Hygiene {hygiene}
Hygiene is how clean he is, from 0 to 100, starting at {START_HYGIENE}.

It drops by {HYGIENE_PER_H} every hour the game is open. Showers, swimming, hotel rooms, the laundry, a spa and the toilets raise it; sweaty jobs and workouts lower it.

When hygiene is under {NEED_AT} he needs a wash more and more (see His needs).

Under {DIRTY_AT} he is dirty, and two things happen. His charm counts less, down to {DIRTY_CHARM_PCT} less at 0 hygiene, which means worse sell prices and fewer charm actions. And his mood drops {DIRTY_MOOD_PER_H} more every hour. The Stats sheet shows charm's drop in red.
# 💪 Attributes
## Strength {str}
Strength starts at 1. It grows a little from physical actions (workouts, hauling, sport), from levelling up, and from gear such as weapons, shields, helmets, boots and backpacks.

Strength unlocks physical actions: for example hauling for the butcher needs 3, arm-wrestling bets 4, loading cargo at the harbour 5, a lifeguard shift 6. Those mostly pay well.

The number counted for requirements is his own strength plus every strength bonus on his gear.
## Charm {cha}
Charm starts at 1. It grows from social actions (chatting, performing, parties), from levelling up, and from gear such as clothes, bracelets, earrings and instruments. A finished city card set adds {SET_CHARM} while he is in that country (see City cards).

Charm unlocks more actions than any other attribute: chatting with the barista needs 3, auditioning at the theatre needs 6, and many social and selling actions need some.

Charm also gets him better prices when he sells: each point adds {SELL_PER_CHA_PCT} to what a shop pays (see The bag and selling).
## Wits {wit}
Wits start at 1. They grow from reading, learning, games, puzzles and city trivia, from levelling up, and from gear such as bows, beanies, wrenches, pocket watches and violins.

Wits unlock clever actions: fixing bikes needs 2, sketching an exhibit or fixing a printer 3, a freelance gig 4, volunteering as a museum guide 6.
## Luck {luck}
Luck starts at 1. It grows a tiny bit from lucky finds, from levelling up, and from gear such as rings, amulets, lucky charms, hoodies and sandals.

Luck does three things:
- Fewer mishaps: each point makes an action's risk {LUCK_RISK_PCT} less likely, up to {LUCK_RISK_CAP_PCT} less. At luck 5 a 20% risk becomes {LUCK5_RISK}.
- Rarer items: each point lifts the shop's rarity roll (see Modifiers and rarity).
- Money on the street: every minute he walks there is a {FIND_PER_LUCK_PCT} chance per point of luck (at most {FIND_CAP_PCT}, more on a leaky-pockets day: see Daily forecast) that he finds {FIND_MIN} to {FIND_MAX}. It shows in his History as "Found €0.50 on the street".
It also unlocks actions that need it, such as finding a coin by the ATM (luck 2). Gear and the Lucky buff count.
# ⭐ Progress
## XP and levels {xp}
He gains XP (experience) from almost every action, and {FIRST_VISIT_XP} XP the first time he reaches each named place. The first-visit bonus isn't changed by buffs, only by the daily forecast (see Daily forecast); XP from actions is multiplied by buffs such as Inspired.

Each level needs 100 x his level in XP: {XP_LV1} XP from level 1 to 2, {XP_LV2} XP from 2 to 3, {XP_LV10} XP from 10 to 11. Extra XP carries over to the next level.

Every time he levels up, his highest attribute (not counting gear) grows by 1, and he gets {SPEED_PER_LEVEL_PCT} faster. He also gets a free pack of city cards (see City cards). On a tie, strength comes first, then charm, wits and luck.

A higher level unlocks better materials for the gear he finds (see Materials) and some actions.
## Fainting and hospital {fainting}
When his health reaches 0 he faints. He wakes up at the nearest hospital the game knows of in the city, or where he is if no hospital has loaded.

Waking up costs {HOSPITAL_FEE}, but never more than {HOSPITAL_MAX_PCT} of his money. His health is set to {WAKE_HEALTH}, and his energy, fullness and thirst are raised to at least 30 if they were lower. He then stays in the hospital for {STAY_MIN} minute before walking on. You'll see "Fainted! Woke up at …" in his History.
# 🎒 Items and gear
## Gear and items {items}
He finds items by browsing shops: weapon and hunting shops sell weapons and armour, hardware stores sell tools he can swing, clothes and shoe shops sell clothes, jewellers and gift shops sell trinkets, music shops sell instruments, and bike shops and vehicle dealers sell vehicles (see Speed and vehicles). Now and then a pet, hardware or carpet shop has a fantasy mount, and a castle's stable sells dragons (see Fantasy mounts).

Each item fits one slot: weapon, offhand, head, body, feet, bag, trinket or vehicle. He wears at most one item per slot. There is no fighting: gear only adds to his attributes, and vehicles to his speed.

Every browse shows him one random item (a bike shop or vehicle dealer shows several: see Saving for a vehicle). He buys it only if it beats what he wears in that slot, meaning its bonuses add up to more, and only if it leaves him at least {KEEP_CENTS}. If he buys it, he puts it on at once and the old item goes into his bag.

An item's name tells you what it is made of: modifiers before, then the material, then the base item, then a suffix, for example "Mighty Bronze Sword of the Fox".
## Materials {materials}
Every item is made of a material, picked at random from those his level has unlocked. Metal items and cloth items use different names for the same steps:
- level 1: Wooden / Cotton (power 1) and Copper / Wool (power 2)
- level 3: Bronze / Linen (power 3)
- level 5: Iron / Leather (power 4)
- level 10: Steel / Silk (power 6)
- level 15: Silver / Velvet (power 7)
- level 25: Mithril / Dragonhide (power 10)

The item's main attribute grows by {POWER_TO_STAT} for each point of power: a Steel Sword gives strength +3. Prices are real: a plain Bronze Sword costs {BRONZE_SWORD}. Material changes the price: Wooden or Cotton is half that, Steel or Silk twice, Mithril or Dragonhide ten times.
## Modifiers and rarity {modifiers}
Items can have 0 to 3 modifiers, and the number decides the rarity:
- Common, no modifiers: {MODS0_PCT} of items
- Uncommon, 1 modifier: {MODS1_PCT}
- Rare, 2 modifiers: {MODS2_PCT}
- Legendary, 3 modifiers: {MODS3_PCT}

Those are the odds at luck 0. Each point of luck lifts the roll by {LUCK_RARITY_PCT}, up to {LUCK_RARITY_CAP_PCT}, so a lucky walker finds rarer items more often.

The first modifier is always a suffix: of the Bear (strength +2), of the Fox (wits +2), of the Peacock (charm +2), of Fortune (luck +2) or of the Owl (wits +1, luck +1). The others are prefixes, never the same one twice: Mighty (strength +2), Charming (charm +2), Clever (wits +2), Lucky (luck +2), Sharp (strength +1, wits +1) or Gleaming (charm +1, luck +1).

Each modifier adds {MOD_PRICE_PCT} of the price.
## The bag and selling {bag}
Gear he takes off goes into his bag, which holds {BAG_SIZE} items. The Stats sheet lists what is in it.

At shops he can "Sell his loot": he sells the cheapest item in his bag. A shop pays {SELL_PCT} of the item's price, plus {SELL_PER_CHA_PCT} of that for every point of charm. For example an item worth €100 sells for {SELL_100_CHA0} with charm 0, or {SELL_100_CHA5} with charm 5. The daily forecast can raise it for a day (see Daily forecast), and a rascal gets more (see Kind or rascal).

While his bag is full he goes on a selling run: resale shops ({RESALE_SHOPS}) pull his next goal like an empty need (see Picking the next goal). There "Sell his loot" sells his cheapest things one after another until {SELL_RUN_LEFT} are left, with a line for each sale. Other shops still buy just one thing at a time.

If his bag is full when another item comes in before he finds such a shop, he sells the cheapest item he may sell on the spot, which can be the new one.

He never sells his {TREASURED_KEEP} most travelled treasured items (see Item stories), unless they are all that is left in the bag.
# ✈ Travel
## Trips to other cities {trips}
Every time he picks a new goal he rolls the trip dice, unless a need is urgent (see His needs):
- {COUNTRY_PCT}: he travels to a city in another country,
- {CITY_PCT}: he travels to another city in the same country,
- otherwise he stays and picks a place in this city.

While no places of note are loaded in the city box, the odds double to {NO_PLACES_COUNTRY_PCT} and {NO_PLACES_CITY_PCT}, so he doesn't stay long in a city where he can only walk to random spots.

The first goal after you choose a city never rolls the dice. The country he is in is the country of the nearest of the {CITY_COUNT} listed cities ({COUNTRY_COUNT} countries). Cities closer than {SAME_CITY_KM} km don't count as a different city. The city is picked at random, weighted by population, so big cities come up most.

While he walks to the station, where he is going stays a surprise: the History says only "somewhere in Bulgaria" or "somewhere abroad", and he reveals the city when he boards. You may guess it (see Guess the next trip). While you are away he doesn't keep it secret.

A trip is never free: he first walks to a bus station, an airport, or (with a car) a gas station, and pays there (see Stations and getting there, and Fares). If there is no way out or he can't pay, he stays, the Debug log says why, and he saves up for it (see Fares).

In the new city he picks goals within 8 x the square root of its population in metres from the centre, at least {MIN_TRIP_CITY_KM} km and at most {MAX_TRIP_CITY_KM} km.
## Stations and getting there {stations}
How he goes depends on the distance in a straight line:
- under {PLANE_FROM_KM} km he always goes by road,
- from {PLANE_FROM_KM} to {PLANE_ALWAYS_KM} km the chance of flying grows steadily,
- over {PLANE_ALWAYS_KM} km he always flies.

By road he takes the bus from the nearest bus station among the places loaded around him. If he owns a car, he walks to the nearest gas station instead (EV chargers don't count), fills up and drives. Roads longer than {MAX_ROAD_KM} km don't count: buses and cars don't cross oceans.

To fly he walks to the nearest airport with scheduled flights within {AIRPORT_KM} km ({AIRPORT_COUNT} airports worldwide, plus the made-up ones of big cities, see Made-up places), and lands at the airport nearest the new city. If there is no airport at either end, he goes by road, and the other way round.

The bus and the car follow the real road route (from the OSRM route planner) at {BUS_KMH} km/h and {CAR_ROAD_KMH} km/h. A plane flies straight at {PLANE_KMH} km/h. These are real speeds, but rides go {RIDE_SPEEDUP} times faster and never take longer than {RIDE_MAX_MIN} minutes of the game being open. So 100 km by bus takes about {BUS_100_MIN} minutes, and 300 km by bus about {BUS_300_MIN}. While he rides, the map zooms out and a red line marks the way. A bus or car arrives in the new city's centre; a plane at its airport. Then the new city's streets load and he walks on.
## Fares {fares}
Fares are in euros and never free (though a rascal may skip a bus ticket: see Kind or rascal):
- bus: {BUS_PER_KM} per km of road,
- plane: {PLANE_BASE} plus {PLANE_PER_KM} per km flown,
- car: fuel at {FUEL_PER_KM} per km of road.

Road distance is the straight line x {ROAD_FACTOR}. So 300 km by bus costs about {BUS_300}, and a 1,500 km flight about {PLANE_1500}, before his souvenirs or the daily forecast make it cheaper (see Souvenirs and Daily forecast).

He pays at the station. If the dice send him on a trip he can't pay for, he stays this time and saves up: until he has the fare, money is a need of at least {SAVING_URGE} urge (see His needs), and spending counts {SAVING_SPEND_PCT} as much. The next trip roll he can pay for takes him. He also saves the same way for his next vehicle (see Saving for a vehicle), so paid work can stay favoured after he has the fare. If he spends too much on the way to the station, he turns back and saves up the same way.
## Country flavour {flavour}
{FLAVOUR_COUNTRIES} countries have their own actions ({FLAVOUR_ACTIONS} in all), such as banitsa with boza at a bakery in Bulgaria or Turkish coffee in a copper pot. They join the usual actions at the matching kind of place while he is in that country.

To make them come up often, each one gets +{FLAVOUR_WEIGHT} weight when he chooses (see How he chooses). They follow the same requirements as every other action.
## City trivia {trivia}
When he reaches a city, the game looks it up on Wikidata: the most populous place within {TRIVIA_KM} km of the centre, and facts about it such as when it was founded, how many people live there, how high it is, up to {TRIVIA_TWINS} twin towns, who it is named after and what it is the capital of.

Each fact becomes a quick action he can do at any place in that city, for example "Learn that Sofia is named after Saint Sofia". Each gives wits +{TRIVIA_WIT} and {TRIVIA_XP} XP. If Wikidata can't be reached, there is simply no trivia for that city.
# 🗂 Collection
## Landmark cards {cards}
The first time he reaches a landmark he gets its card. Landmarks are places of these kinds: {LANDMARK_KINDS}. Art shops don't count.

A card's rarity comes from the place's rank on the map (see Places of note):
- rank {CARD_RARE_RANK}, the most important in its area: Rare,
- rank {CARD_UNCOMMON_RANK}: Uncommon,
- any other: Common.

On top of its rarity a card can have a finish: {GOLD_PCT} of cards are Gold and {FOIL_PCT} are Foil. Each landmark has one card, given on his first visit; going back gives nothing more, and a card's finish never changes. The feed says "New card", and the Collection sheet shows them all.
## City cards {citycards}
City cards are trading cards of the world's cities: the {CITY_CARDS_PER_COUNTRY} biggest cities of every country, {CITY_CARD_TOTAL} cards in all. A card's rarity is fixed by how big its city is in its country:
- the biggest: Rare,
- the next {UNCOMMON_CITY_COUNT}: Uncommon,
- the rest: Common.

Cards come in packs of {PACK_SIZE}. He gets a free pack each time he levels up, and for each new city and each new country he reaches, and opens them at his next stop. He also buys packs at {CARD_SHOPS}: "Buy a city card pack" costs {PACK_PRICE}. Each card in a pack is Rare {PULL_RARE_PCT} of the time, Uncommon {PULL_UNCOMMON_PCT} and Common {PULL_COMMON_PCT}, then a random city of that rarity. Like landmark cards, a card can be Foil or Gold.

A card he already has is a spare, unless it has a better finish: then it takes the old card's place and the old one is the spare. He sells his spares the next time he reaches one of those shops: {SPARE_COMMON} for a Common, {SPARE_UNCOMMON} for an Uncommon and {SPARE_RARE} for a Rare, {FOIL_MULT} times that if Foil and {GOLD_MULT} times if Gold.

When he has every card of a country, its set is done: he gets Charm +{SET_CHARM} while he is in that country. At made-up places (see While you were away) he doesn't sell spares or open free packs; those wait for his next real stop.
## Placedex {placedex}
The Placedex counts every kind of place he has been to, such as a cafe, a Christian place of worship or a viewpoint. The first time he reaches a kind he has never seen, he gets bonus XP by how rare that kind is:
- Common: +{DEX_XP_COMMON} XP,
- Uncommon: +{DEX_XP_UNCOMMON} XP,
- Rare: +{DEX_XP_RARE} XP,
- Legendary: +{DEX_XP_LEGENDARY} XP.

Rarity comes from a POI sweep of big cities around the world: a kind found {DEX_COMMON_MIN} or more times there is Common, {DEX_UNCOMMON_MIN} or more Uncommon, {DEX_RARE_MIN} or more Rare, and anything rarer, or never found at all, is Legendary. The sweep knows {DEX_KINDS} kinds. This XP comes on top of the first-visit XP and isn't changed by buffs or mood.
## Souvenirs {souvenirs}
Every country has one souvenir for him, and it costs nothing. Each time he reaches a landmark (see Landmark cards) in a country he has no souvenir from, there is a {SOUVENIR_PCT} chance he finds it there. {SOUVENIR_COUNTRIES} countries have their own souvenir, such as a bottle of rose oil from Bulgaria; anywhere else it is a fridge magnet.

Souvenirs make him a seasoned traveller: every {SOUVENIRS_PER_CUT} souvenirs make his fares {FARE_CUT_PCT} cheaper, up to {FARE_CUT_MAX_PCT} (see Fares).
## Crawls {crawls}
A crawl is a little set he finishes by visiting different places of a kind. Each is done once, and from then on it gives a small lasting perk every time he reaches one of its places, on top of whatever he does there:
{CRAWL_LIST}

"A day" is a day on your device's clock, so a crawl he hasn't finished by midnight starts over. "In one city" starts over when he travels to another city. Visiting the same place twice counts once.
## Item stories {stories}
Everything he buys remembers its story: the city he bought it in and when, every country he has been to while wearing it, and how far he has walked in it. Tap an item on the Stats sheet to read it.

An item worn through {TREASURED_COUNTRIES} or more countries becomes treasured (💎). He never sells his {TREASURED_KEEP} most travelled treasured items from his bag (see The bag and selling). Gear he had before stories existed has none.
# ☘ Little touches
## Clovers and care packages {care}
You never steer him, but you can leave him a small gift. Gifts cost clovers, and you earn clovers just by checking in:
- opening the game (or coming back to its tab) after at least {CLOVER_AWAY_H} hour away earns {CLOVERS_PER_CHECKIN} ☘ clover,
- at most {CLOVERS_PER_DAY} clovers a day, counted by your device's date,
- there are no streaks: a day you skip costs nothing, and clovers never run out.

☰ Menu → 🎁 Care package shows your clovers and what they buy. Tap a gift's ☘ button to send it. One package is on its way at a time, and he opens it at the next place he reaches (also while the game is closed) and thanks you in his History:
{GIFT_LIST}

A boost works like any buff (see Buffs and debuffs). Gifts are optional: he never waits for one. A right guess about his next trip earns clovers too (see Guess the next trip). Each package he opens adds to his trust in you (see His trust in you).
## His trust in you {trust}
He doesn't know who you are, but he can tell someone is looking out for him. His trust in you runs from 0 to {TRUST_MAX} and starts at {TRUST_START}. The Care package sheet shows it.
- A check-in that earns a clover adds {TRUST_PER_CHECKIN}, and each care package he opens adds {TRUST_PER_GIFT}.
- Staying away fades it by {TRUST_FADE_PER_DAY} for each whole day after the first, but never by more than {TRUST_FADE_MAX} at once, so it never drops sharply.

Each time his trust passes {LETTER_AT}, he writes you a letter in his History (it starts with ✉). After opening a package he sometimes writes a thank-you too, about {THANKS_PCT} of the time. The more he trusts you, the more likely he listens to a whisper (see Whispers).
## Whispers {whisper}
Up to {WHISPERS_PER_DAY} times a day (by your device's date) you can whisper one word to him from the Care package sheet. He listens with a chance of {HEED_MIN_PCT}, plus {HEED_PER_TRUST_PCT} for each point of trust, so {HEED_FULL_PCT} at full trust. Otherwise he shrugs it off. Either way his History says so. When he listens, he follows the whisper for {NUDGE_MIN} minutes:
- 🍞 Eat: he acts as if he is starving and thirsty: food and drink are all he looks for, the nearest first, and he won't start a new trip (see His needs).
- 💤 Rest: he acts as if he has no energy left: coffee, benches and naps are all he looks for, the nearest first, and he won't start a new trip.
- 🧭 Explore: places he has never been to pull him {EXPLORE_PULL} times as hard.
- 💰 Save: he saves as if for a trip: paid work counts more and spending less (see How he chooses).

Whispers are always optional, and they never use up clovers. His dice for whether he listens are separate from his walk's, so a whisper changes only what he would have done next.
## Kind or rascal {lean}
He leans kind or rascal, from {LEAN_MIN} to +{LEAN_MAX}, starting in the middle. The Care package sheet shows his lean and his latest line (not counting whispers and letters), with 👍 Praise and 👎 Scold buttons. Praise moves him {JUDGE_STEP} towards kind and scold {JUDGE_STEP} towards rascal, once per line. Left alone he drifts by his own deeds: each kind deed ({KIND_DEEDS}) moves him {DEED_STEP} towards kind, and each rascal deed ({RASCAL_DEEDS}) {DEED_STEP} towards rascal.

From {LEAN_AT} either way it shows:
- Kind: kind deeds count +{KIND_DEED_WEIGHT} in How he chooses, so he does them more.
- Rascal: things he sells fetch {RASCAL_SELL_PCT} more, and on a bus he skips the ticket {DODGE_PCT} of the time. A skipped ticket is caught {CAUGHT_PCT} of the time, and then he pays {FINE_MULT} times the fare as a fine (never more than he has).
## Guess the next trip {guess}
When the trip dice send him to another city, the top card shows "🎲 Where to?" with up to {GUESS_CHOICES} choices while he walks to the station:
- for a trip in his country, the right city and other cities of the country,
- for a trip abroad, the right country and other countries.

The city he is in is never a choice. When the bundled city list has no other city to offer, there are fewer choices, or no guess for that trip.

Tap one to guess. You get one guess per trip. When he boards, his History says whether you were right, and a right guess earns {GUESS_CLOVERS} ☘ clover (see Clovers and care packages), on top of the daily ones. If you don't guess, nothing changes: he goes all the same.

If the trip is called off before he boards (he can't pay, or he faints), the guess goes with it. So does leaving the game for {MIN_AWAY_MIN} minute or more before he boards: the catch-up starts him afresh at a place (see While you were away). Trips he takes while you are away have no guess.
## Postcards to friends {postcards}
☰ Menu → 📮 Send a postcard writes a card of where he is right now: the city, the place or street he is at, his level and the last thing he did. Sign it with your name if you like (up to {POSTCARD_NAME_MAX} letters; the game remembers it, even through New city), then tap 📤 Share postcard. Your phone's share sheet opens; where there is none, the link is copied, or shown for you to copy.

The whole card travels inside the link, after the #, so no server keeps it. When a friend opens it, their game shows "A postcard from Ivan in Sofia" (your name, or "a friend" if you left it empty), right away on the city picker, or in a game once its While you were away card and any open sheet are closed, with 📮 Send one back if they have a game going, or Start my own walk if they don't. Opening a postcard changes nothing in their game.
## One choice on reopen {dilemma}
Now and then, when you open the game after at least {DILEMMA_AWAY_H} hours away ({DILEMMA_PCT} of such reopens), the While you were away card holds one small choice, such as "A trucker offers a free ride to Varna." Tap an option and it happens. Or just carry on: when the card closes, or you leave the game with it open, he decides by himself, and his History says "Decided by himself". Nothing waits for you.

When he decides, it's even odds, tilted toward the first option by {LEAN_PCT} for each point of the attribute he leans on above 1, up to {LEAN_CAP_PCT}. Below 1 it stays even. The choices:
{DILEMMA_LIST}

At chess he wins {CHESS_BASE_PCT} of the time, plus {CHESS_PER_WIT_PCT} for each point of Wits, up to {CHESS_MAX_PCT}.

A free ride takes him there at once and at no cost, unlike a normal trip (see Trips to other cities). There are no free rides while he is on a paid one; if he boards one while the card is open, he turns the free ride down himself. A free ride replaces a trip he is still walking to the station for, and its guess.
# 📱 Screens and buttons
## The top card and buttons {screen}
The card at the top shows the city and what he is doing, his money and level, the street he is on (or the ride he is on), his goal with the distance and the approximate walking time, the "🎲 Where to?" guess while he walks to a station for a trip, today's forecast in one dim line (see Daily forecast), and three small bars: ❤ health, ⚡ energy and 🍞 fullness. While he rests it shows the countdown and a bar. A red line in it warns about anything that could leave him stuck; tap it to open Debug.

What he just did pops up under it for a few seconds.

Only three buttons sit on the map, at the bottom right in reach of your thumb: + and − to zoom, and ☰ for the menu. The small ⓘ at the bottom left shows the map credits. "Back to him" shows up at the bottom when you have dragged the map away while the camera follows him.
## The menu {menu}
☰ Menu (bottom right) opens from the bottom of the screen and holds History, Stats, Collection, Care package (with your clovers), Send a postcard, Help, Debug, the Route to goal, Camera follows him, City area and City sound switches, and New city. Tap an entry to open it; ✕ Close or the Escape key closes it again.

To see more of the map, drag any sheet down by its title bar (the one with the grip line). Camera follows him (a switch, on at first and remembered) keeps him in view: the map glides over to him whenever he walks out of the middle of the screen. Turn it off and the map stays wherever you leave it, even when he walks off the screen (see The map).

Route to goal (a switch, off at first and remembered) draws his planned route in orange: a solid line along the streets he will take, then a dashed straight line for the last part where the streets are not loaded yet.

City sound (a switch, off at first and remembered) plays a soft loop for where he is (see City sound).
## The Stats sheet {sheet}
🧍 Stats opens his character sheet, a set of cards:
- Level, his title once he has one (see Badges and titles), an XP bar towards the next level, his money, his speed and how mood scales his XP.
- Saving for: the vehicle he saves up for (see Saving for a vehicle) and any trip fare, each with a gold bar of how much he has.
- Vitals: a coloured bar and the value for each of his six meters.
- Attributes: a tile for each, with the part his gear, buffs and a finished city card set add in green.
- Gear: every slot, with what he wears in it coloured by rarity (grey Common, green Uncommon, blue Rare, orange Legendary), or Empty. Tap an item he bought to see its story (see Item stories); 💎 marks a treasured one, and its story says if he won't sell it.
- Bag: what he carries, out of {BAG_SIZE}. Tap the Bag heading to hide or show the list, and an item to see its story.
- Effects: his buffs with the minutes they have left.
## The Collection sheet {collection}
🗂 Collection (in the menu) shows everything he collects. You never have to open it: he collects on his own.

At the top, Completion counts every tile he has found and every badge tier he holds, out of all of them. Under it are his badges and his title (see Badges and titles). Then come five tabs:
- 🎴 Cards: landmark cards (see Landmark cards), newest first (the newest {CARDS_SHOWN}), and city cards with a row per country he has cards from.
- 📇 Places: the Placedex by rarity, the odd places the game makes up (see Made-up places) and the crawls.
- 🌍 World: every country, how many cities he has been to, and the souvenirs.
- ⚔ Gear: every item base (such as a Sword or a Coat), every material and every vehicle he has owned.
- 🏅 Feats: his badges, a card for each tab they belong to.

Each set has a bar and a grid of tiles, like a dex. A found tile shows its name, framed in its rarity colour (double for Foil or Gold). One he hasn't found yet is a ?. Tap a tile: a found one shows its detail, and a ? shows a hint, such as "Found in France" or "A Rare kind of shop". Landmark cards have no end, so they don't count towards Completion. An item counts once he wears or carries it, for its base and its material.
## Badges and titles {badges}
A feat counts something he does. Its first number gives him a 🥉 Bronze badge, the second 🥈 Silver and the third 🥇 Gold. The feed says so, and a badge stays even if the count drops later (say he sells a treasured item). A Gold badge also gives him a title, shown on the Collection and Stats sheets: the newest one he earned. Badges and titles give nothing else.

## History {history}
📜 History lists his last {HISTORY_SIZE} events, newest first, each with the time it happened: arrivals, actions, purchases, level-ups, trips and faints. That includes what he did while you were away, at the times it happened.

While it is open, the map shows the trail he walked (up to about {TRAIL_KM} km), with numbered markers where things happened. Events at the same spot share one marker, such as "4-6". Tap an event to zoom the map to where it happened. Trips break the trail, because he didn't walk them.
## Debug {debug}
Debug (in the menu) is for testing and curious players.

Severe problems are ones that could leave him stuck. Once the streets all around him have loaded, the game checks the city box for a way out (a bus station, an airport or a gas station) and for a place that pays him. Missing ones are normally made up (see Made-up places), so this only shows when that fails, for example when the game is offline before it has kept that part of its code. If one is missing, a red ⛔ line stays in the top card until the city has it or he leaves. A city without a gas station is flagged too.

At the top of Debug, coloured boxes list what looks wrong with the city right now. Cards below show the city, what has loaded (street squares, places, trivia), the ways out and places that pay, and the trip dice.

Then come three logs, closed at first: the city's trivia, Problems (the last {MAX_SEVERE} severe problems, {MAX_ERRORS} errors and {MAX_WARNINGS} warnings from anywhere in the game) and Goal picks (his last {DEBUG_SIZE} goals). Each entry is a short title, such as "🎯 Chosen: Cafe East". Tap it to see how it happened: every dice roll, the odds and why. A random spot always counts as a warning. A red dot on ☰ and Debug means there are problems you haven't looked at yet. Problems are kept even through New city.

City area (a switch in the menu) shades the box his goals are picked from in orange and draws the city's real border from OpenStreetMap in purple, so you can see where the two differ. The switch is remembered.

Its buttons change the game right away:
- New goal ends any stop or trip and picks a new goal now, rolling the trip dice again.
- New city sends him straight to another city in the same country.
- New country sends him straight to a city in another country.

The 🤔 Dilemmas switch, "One on every long reopen", makes every reopen after {DILEMMA_AWAY_H} hours bring a choice (see One choice on reopen), for testing. The switch is remembered.
## New city {new_city}
New city (in the menu) starts the whole game over in a city you choose. Because it wipes everything (stats, money, gear, bag, History and the goal-pick log), you have to type RESET in capitals and confirm.

To just see him somewhere else without losing anything, wait for a trip, or use the Debug buttons.
## About Help {help}
Help (in the menu) explains every part of the game on its own page. Tap a topic to open it and Back to return to the list.

To find something fast, type in the search box at the top. Results appear as you type: every page that holds all the words you typed, in any order, with pages whose title matches first. Words are matched from their start ("fast" finds "faster", but "fee" doesn't find "coffee"), and capitals and accents don't matter ("cafe" finds "Café"). Each result shows the line where your word was found, with the word marked. Tap one to open the page; Back brings you to your results, and ✖ clears the box and shows the full list again.

The numbers on these pages come straight from the game, so they are always the ones it really uses. Help is updated whenever a mechanic changes.
