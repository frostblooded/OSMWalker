# ℹ Getting started
## What OSM Walker is {about}
OSM Walker is a little character who walks the real streets of a real city, taken from OpenStreetMap. The whole game is one choice, made again and again: where does he go next? At each place he gets what it gives him, and you pick his next place from {CARDS} cards. Work places pay him money; sights and green spaces give him XP, and enough XP takes him up a level. Money buys upgrades at real shops among the cards, and each one changes what the cards can offer: a 4th card, a bike, a metro ride across town, a train to another city.

The game runs while it is open on screen. While it is closed, time stops and he waits where he was (see Real time).

Each page in this Help explains one part of the game, with the real numbers the game uses.
## Choosing a city {city_search}
On the first launch you pick where he starts. Start typing the name of a town or city. After {SEARCH_MIN_CHARS} letters and a short pause, suggestions appear by themselves; you can also tap 🔍 or press Enter to search right away. Only towns, cities, villages and suburbs are suggested, and missing accents or small typos are fine ("compiegne" finds Compiegne). Tap a suggestion to start there.

If the search servers can't be reached, it says "Couldn't reach the search server": check your connection and try again. "Nothing found" means there really is no such place. Answers are remembered until you close the game, so searching the same name again is instant.

The game then downloads the streets around that spot. He starts on the street corner nearest to the result and stays inside that city.

The area of the result decides the city box his cards come from. It is grown or shrunk so that it reaches at least {MIN_CITY_KM} km and at most {MAX_CITY_KM} km from the centre, so a tiny village still has room to walk and a whole country never counts as one city.
## Real time {offline}
While the game is open on screen he lives in real time: one minute on your clock is one minute for him. A single step never counts for more than {MAX_STEP_S} second of your time, so a stall or a hidden tab can't make him jump ahead.

When you close the game or hide the tab, time stops: he waits where he is and goes on from there when you come back. Nothing is lost while it is closed.

The game saves every {SAVE_EVERY_S} seconds and when you leave, on this device only. Clearing the browser's site data starts you over. If a save can't be read, the game says so and starts over, and keeps a copy of the old save on the device in case it can be fixed.
# 🚶 Walking
## How he walks {walking}
He walks at {WALK_KMH} km/h, about {WALK_M_PER_MIN} m a minute, along real streets, paths and footways. Motorways and other roads you can't walk on are left out. With the 🚲 Bike he rides at {BIKE_KMH} km/h and with the 🛵 Scooter at {SCOOTER_KMH} km/h, on the same streets (see Upgrades).

He walks to the place you picked (see Where next). He plans the shortest route over the streets that are loaded so far, and plans again as more streets load. If the streets ahead of him are not downloaded yet, he stands at the edge and "Loading streets…" shows at the top until they arrive.

If he has walked more than {TRIP_RATIO} times the straight-line distance to his goal plus {TRIP_EXTRA_KM} km and still isn't there, the goal is probably across water or behind a fence, so he gives up on it, gets nothing, and you get new cards (the feed says so). After more than {MAX_SKIPS} give-ups in a row he just walks down a random street for a while.
## Places and rewards {places}
Only named places that give him something are offered, and each kind gives one thing:
- 💶 work places pay money: {WORK_LIST},
- ⭐ sights give XP: {SIGHT_LIST},
- 🌳 green spaces give less XP: {GREEN_LIST}.

The longer the walk, the bigger the reward. A minute of walking is worth {WORK_PER_MIN} at a work place, {SIGHT_XP_PER_MIN} XP at a sight and {GREEN_XP_PER_MIN} XP in a green space, and every stop is worth {STOP_MIN} minutes on top. So a work place {EX_NEAR_M} m away pays {EX_NEAR_EURO} and one {EX_FAR_KM} km away {EX_FAR_EURO}. A close place pays more for each minute, so it is best while you keep picking; a far one pays more in all, so it is best when you'll look away for a while. The top {LANDMARK_RANK} sights in their area are landmarks and give {LANDMARK_X} times the XP.

Rewards count the walk on foot, even on a bike or scooter, so riding brings in more for each minute of your time.

The first time he reaches a place he gets {NEW_PLACE_XP} XP on top, and its card says 🆕 New. A place is remembered by its name and where it is. He counts as arrived when he is within {ARRIVE_M} m of it; what he got shows in the feed under the top card and as a 💶 or ⭐ over him for {POSE_S} seconds.

The reward on a card is fixed when the card is dealt, and it is what he gets there, however the walk turns out.
## Where next {goal}
When he reaches a place he stops, and 🧭 Where next? shows at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to see {CARDS} cards ({MAP_CARDS} with the 🗺 City map), each a place nearby with what it gives (an icon and the exact number) and how far it is in metres and walking minutes. Orange pins numbered like the cards mark their places on the map. Tap a card and he sets off. He waits for you as long as you like; nothing is lost while he waits. If he can't get to the place you picked (it is across water, say), he gives up, gets nothing there, and you pick again.

The cards are a planned mix:
- a close one, at most {CLOSE_MAX_M} m away when there is one (else the nearest), which pays less,
- a far one, at least {FAR_MIN_M} m away when there is one (else the farthest left), which pays more,
- and one of a kind the other two aren't, if there is one (a green space when they are a cafe and a sight), a place he has never been to when possible.

Upgrades bring cards of their own, which take the place of that last card: a shop selling the next upgrade first, then a 📕 never-seen sight, a 📦 parcel, a 🚇 metro ride or a 🚆 train, in a random order (see Upgrades). Under the cards a line says what the next upgrade needs.

They come from places within {REACH_KM} km of him ({BIKE_REACH_KM} km on a bike, {SCOOTER_REACH_KM} km on a scooter, and the close and far distances stretch the same way); when fewer than {CARDS} are, from the {NEAREST} nearest. Places from his last {RECENT} picks don't come back, and important places turn up more often. The walking time is a guess: streets wind, so it counts {ROUTE_FACTOR} times the straight line.

When there is no card to deal (right after starting in a new city, before any places have loaded, or when every place near him was one of his last {RECENT} picks), he walks to a random spot near him instead, more likely ahead than behind, and gets nothing there.

While he walks, the card at the top shows where he is going, how far it is and how long the walk will take, for example "🎯 Going to Central Market · 1.2 km · ~14 min". The time is the planned route at his speed. A 🎯 pin on the map marks the goal. Tap that line to see him and his goal on the map together; tap it again to go back to him.
## The map {map}
The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenFreeMap; the ⓘ at the bottom left shows the credits when you tap it. He is the round orange face 🙂, with a bubble over his head showing the kind of place he is heading for (☕ a cafe, 🏛 a museum). While he waits for your pick, orange circles numbered like the cards mark the places on the cards.

While the camera follows him (the Camera follows him switch in the menu, on at first) the map stays still while he walks around the middle, and glides over to him once he is more than {MIDDLE_PCT}% of the view away from the middle. That way the whole map is only redrawn every so often, which saves battery. Drag the map to look around, then tap "Back to him" to follow him again. With the switch off the map never moves by itself.

Use the + and − buttons on the right to zoom in and out; the zoom is remembered. Streets are downloaded in squares around him, {STREET_SQUARES} by {STREET_SQUARES} map tiles of about {TILE_KM} km each (smaller far from the equator), and the squares in the direction he is heading load first. Streets he has seen are kept on the device for {CACHE_DAYS} days, so the same city loads faster next time and works with a weak connection.
# ⭐ Progress
## Money {coins}
Money is shown in euros on the top card. He earns it at work places, more the farther he walked (see Places and rewards), and with the 📦 Courier bag by delivering parcels. He spends it on upgrades at shops (see Upgrades).
## XP and levels {xp}
He gains XP from sights and green spaces, and {NEW_PLACE_XP} XP the first time he reaches any place (see Places and rewards).

Each level needs {XP_PER_LEVEL} x his level in XP: {XP_LV1} XP from level 1 to 2, {XP_LV2} XP from 2 to 3, {XP_LV10} XP from 10 to 11. Extra XP carries over to the next level, and each level-up shows in the feed. Some levels open the shop for an upgrade, and the feed says which: "Reached level {BIKE_LEVEL}: a bike shop now sells a 🚲 Bike ({BIKE_EURO})", when it is the next one to buy.
## Upgrades {upgrades}
There are {LADDER_SIZE} upgrades, bought in this order:
{LADDER}

Each upgrade opens at its level. Once he has the money too, a shop selling it turns up among the cards, with a gold tint and its price. He buys it when he gets there, and it works from the very next cards. A shop of its own kind is used when one is near; otherwise any shop sells it (but only a station sells the metro pass and the train ticket), as far as {SHOP_REACH_KM} km away. The Stats sheet lists them all.

📕 Guidebook: one card is a sight he has never been to, for {GUIDE_XP_X} times its XP (and the first-visit bonus on top).

📦 Parcels: he picks the parcel up at a work place close by, then goes on by himself to the drop-off, a work place farther off, and is paid there: {PARCEL_PAY_X} times what the walk from pick-up to drop-off would pay. The card shows both places, and its distance and time count both walks.

🚇 Metro: he goes to a station near him and rides to a far part of the city, at least {METRO_MIN_KM} km away, with fresh places around. The ride takes no time and pays nothing by itself (a station he has never been to gives the first-visit bonus, like shops do); the card says where it goes.

🚆 Train: he goes to a station near him, and there you pick any town or city, as when you started. He keeps his money, level, upgrades and places, and History shows the trip. "Stay in …" keeps him where he is and deals new cards. If the streets there can't load, he stays at the station and you can try again.
# 📱 Screens and buttons
## The top card and buttons {screen}
The card at the top shows the city, the street he is on, his money and level, and his goal with the distance and the walking time. What he just did pops up under it for a few seconds.

Only a few buttons sit on the map, at the bottom in reach of your thumb: + and − to zoom and ☰ for the menu on the right, and 🧭 Where next? while he waits for your pick (see Where next), or 🚆 Where to by train? at a station (see Upgrades). "Back to him" shows up at the bottom when the map has moved away from him while the camera follows him.
## The menu {menu}
☰ Menu (bottom right) opens from the bottom of the screen and holds History, Stats, Help, Debug, the Route to goal, Camera follows him and City area switches, and New city. Tap an entry to open it; ✕ Close or the Escape key closes it again.

To see more of the map, drag any sheet down by its title bar. It stays at the height where you let go, up to {SHEET_TALL_PCT}% of the screen (all of it for full-screen sheets like Help). Let go below {SHEET_CLOSE_PCT}% of the screen and it closes.

Route to goal (a switch, off at first and remembered) draws his planned route in orange: a solid line along the streets he will take, then a dashed straight line for the last part where the streets are not loaded yet.

Camera follows him (a switch, on at first and remembered) keeps him in view (see The map).
## The Stats sheet {sheet}
🧍 Stats shows his level with a bar for the XP to the next one, his money, and how many places he has been to. Below it, his upgrades: the ones he owns, the next one with a bar to its price once its level is reached, and the rest with the level they open at.
## History {history}
📜 History lists his last {HISTORY_SIZE} events, newest first, each with the time it happened: setting off, the places he reached with what he got, level-ups, upgrades bought, parcels, and metro and train rides.

While it is open, the map shows the trail he walked (up to about {TRAIL_KM} km), with numbered markers where things happened. Events at the same spot share one marker, such as "4-6". Tap an event to zoom the map to where it happened, then "Back to him" (while the camera follows him) to follow him again.
## Debug {debug}
Debug (in the menu) is for testing and curious players.

At the top, coloured boxes list what looks wrong with the city right now. Cards below show the city (its box, where he is and what he is doing) and what has loaded. 🃏 New cards drops his goal and deals new cards at the next corner.

Then come two logs, closed at first: Problems (the last {MAX_ERRORS} errors and {MAX_WARNINGS} warnings from anywhere in the game) and Cards and picks (the last {DEBUG_SIZE} deals, picks and random spots). Each entry is a short title, such as "🎯 Picked 2 of 3: Cafe East". Tap it to see the detail: each card's place, kind, slot (close, far, other, shop, guide, parcel, metro or train), distance and reward, or why a random spot was used. A random spot counts as a warning. A red dot on ☰ and Debug means there are problems you haven't looked at yet. Problems are kept even through New city.

City area (a switch in the menu) shades the box his cards come from in orange and draws the city's real border from OpenStreetMap in purple, so you can see where the two differ. The switch is remembered.
## New city {new_city}
New city (in the menu) starts the whole game over in a city you choose. Because it wipes everything (money, level, upgrades, places visited, History, the cards and their log), you have to type RESET in capitals and confirm.
## About Help {help}
Help (in the menu) explains every part of the game on its own page. Tap a topic to open it and Back to return to the list.

To find something fast, type in the search box at the top. Results appear as you type: every page that holds all the words you typed, in any order, with pages whose title matches first. Words are matched from their start ("fast" finds "faster", but "fee" doesn't find "coffee"), and capitals and accents don't matter ("cafe" finds "Café"). Each result shows the line where your word was found, with the word marked. Tap one to open the page; Back brings you to your results, and ✖ clears the box and shows the full list again.

The numbers on these pages come straight from the game, so they are always the ones it really uses. Help is updated whenever a mechanic changes.
