# Architecture map

Where things live, and the links between files that no import shows. Every
source file opens with a comment saying what it does; read that before the
code.

## Layers

Imports only point down this list (`src/sw/` imports nothing).

| Layer | Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screens | `src/ui/` | Preact screens and sheets. `kit.tsx` + `app.css` are the UI kit (see `docs/ui-guidelines.md`). `help.ts` fills Help text with numbers from `src/core` (and `snap.ts`); `HelpSheet.tsx` loads it as its own chunk, off the startup path, and the text when Help first opens. |
| App | `src/app/` | `loop.ts` ticks the game while the page is visible. `data.ts` fetches `public/data/` once. `sound.ts` is the optional city sound, made with Web Audio and loaded only when it is turned on. |
| Map | `src/map/` | `scene.ts` decides what the map shows and where the camera points. `mapView.ts` is the only file that talks to MapLibre. `icons.ts` holds the game's own map icons as SVG text, a lazy chunk loaded the first time an icon shows. |
| Game | `src/game/` | `game.ts` runs a city: walker, streets, trips, saves, and the catch-up on reopen (`wake()`). `journey.ts` trips, `saveFile.ts` saves and settings, `hud.ts` what the screens show. |
| Streets | `src/streets/` | The Web Worker that fetches, decodes and caches street tiles (`source.ts`), and `loader.ts`, which picks which tiles to load next. |
| Network | `src/net/api.ts` | The game's outside services: OpenFreeMap street tiles, Overpass, Photon, Nominatim, OSRM, Wikidata, Open-Meteo weather, Nager.Date public holidays. The map style and map tiles are fetched by `src/map/mapView.ts` and MapLibre itself. |
| Core | `src/core/` | Pure logic with no DOM and no network: walker, route (A*), goal weights, stats, items, trips, history, keepsakes (landmark cards, Placedex, souvenirs, crawls), the Collection's sets, feats and badges (`collection.ts`), money in cents, a seedable RNG, the sky (`sky.ts`: sun, season, the city's clock and what weather does; `weather.ts`: Open-Meteo forecasts; `opening.ts`: opening hours), clovers and care packages (`gifts.ts`), golden bricks and the monuments they buy (`bricks.ts`), place creatures (`creatures.ts`: a troll at bridges, a sprite at fountains, a dryad in parks), ley lines, Magic and spells (`magic.ts`), the shop after midnight and its trinkets (`midnight.ts`; its enchantments live with the other modifiers in `items.ts`), portals at old monuments and castle ruins to a city he has never been to (`portals.ts`; the jump is a `portal` ride in `trips.ts`), giants seen from afar at waterfronts and viewpoints (`giants.ts`), naps in fairy rings at dawn (`fairy.ts`), the travel kit he packs for a trip (`travelKit.ts`), border lines on entering a country (`border.ts`), his life story (`chronicle.ts`), his trust in you, whispers and kind-or-rascal lean (`bond.ts`), timed moments (`moments.ts`: a choice or mini-game with a timer and his own pick; `placeMoments.ts`: one per kind of place and a few for trips, with what each pick does; `minigames.ts`: the four mini-games' rules), postcards packed into a link (`postcard.ts`), the one choice a long reopen may hold (`dilemmas.ts`), his look on the map (`look.ts`: face, bubble, pose and place icons), today's forecast out of `data/forecasts.json` (`forecast.ts`), deadpan jokes on feed lines (`jokes.ts`), his week's tally for the recap (`week.ts`), the weekly bills and the debt collector (`bills.ts`, paid when `Week.roll` starts a week), his life records (`records.ts`), the storyteller that paces incidents and the incident deck it deals from, night legends among them (`storyteller.ts`, `incidents.ts`), Viktor, his rival (`rival.ts`), his friends, partner and travel companions (`friends.ts`), the clubs friends bring him into (`clubs.ts`), friends' news and moves (`townies.ts`), his birthday and month marks (`anniversaries.ts`), his pet: a stray that stays, grows and may turn out a fairy creature (`pet.ts`), chance cards at work (`workCards.ts`), which soundscape plays (`soundscape.ts`), the city's made-up places (`poiGen.ts`: must-haves, extras, wizard towers) and which places get map icons (`mapIcons.ts`), his quests and the world events near him (`quests.ts`), and `catchup.ts`, which lives through the time the game was closed without walking the map. |
| Offline | `src/sw/` | The service worker and its cache rules, built on its own. |

## Hidden links

- **Help text.** `public/data/help.txt` holds `{PLACEHOLDERS}`, and `src/ui/help.ts` fills them from core constants. `vite.config.ts` hashes the file into `__HELP_VERSION__`, which goes in its address (`HelpSheet.tsx` `HELP_URL`), so a deploy that changes Help gives it a new address. The service worker serves a `data/` file with `?v=` cache-first and deletes its older versions when it stores a new one (`src/sw/strategy.ts` `replaced`). `tests/unit/help.test.ts` fails on a missing or unused value, or on a meter, attribute, slot or screen button with no page.
- **Quests.** `public/data/quests.json` is the quest and event bank, read through `src/app/data.ts`; its place keys must be `actions.json` keys (`tests/unit/quests.test.ts` checks). The game and `catchup.ts` both call `questGoal` before a goal and `questStop` at each stop (with `stats.sky` fresh first: `questGoal` and `startEvent` skip closed places by it), and both skip the trip dice while he is on a quest, so a watched and an unwatched day match. Quest and event icons ride the map icons layer (`game.questIcons()`; the scene's icon key holds `quests.rev`).
- **Game data.** `public/data/actions.json`, `incidents.json` (the incident deck; `tests/unit/storyteller.test.ts` checks its place classes and attributes), `rival.json` (what he and Viktor compete for; `tests/unit/rival.test.ts` checks it), `friends.json` (names, faces, fellow travellers and friends' news; `tests/unit/friends.test.ts` and `tests/unit/townies.test.ts` check it), `creatures.json` (the place creatures; `tests/unit/creatures.test.ts` checks their place classes and that there are as many as the Folklore feat's Gold tier), `spells.json` (the spells; `tests/unit/magic.test.ts` checks their meters and that there are as many as the Spellbook feat's Gold tier), `flavour.json`, `cities.tsv`, `airports.tsv`, `placedex.json`, `jokes.json`, `forecasts.json`, `letters.json`, `borders.json` (border words by country code; `tests/unit/border.test.ts` checks they cover every `flavour.json` country) and `words.json` (local words by language, each country's language, and the place classes each meaning is learned at; `tests/unit/words.test.ts` checks the codes and classes) are read through `src/app/data.ts`. `tools/` rebuilds `cities.tsv`, runs the POI sweep (`.github/workflows/poi-sweep.yml`) and turns a sweep report into `placedex.json` (`make_placedex.py`). Its `BANDS` are copied by `DEX_BANDS` in `src/core/keepsakes.ts` for Help, and its `SKIPPED` copies `NOT_A_PLACE_SUBCLASS` in `src/core/ofm.ts`; `tests/unit/keepsakes.test.ts` checks both copies.
- **Saves.** Changing a saved field means a repair step on the way in from `src/game/saveFile.ts`, because players keep old saves. Stats fields are repaired in `Stats.fromJSON` (and `Keepsakes.fromJSON`), which `saveFile.ts` calls; `tests/unit/saveFile.test.ts` loads an old save through it. A save that can't be read or repaired is copied to `osmwalker.save.bad` (`BAD_SAVE_KEY`) before the new game overwrites it. Public holiday lists sit apart under `HOLIDAYS_KEY` (fetched once per country per year); `src/core/iso2.ts` must keep a two-letter code for every country code in `cities.tsv`, or that country gets no holidays (`tests/unit/holidays.test.ts` checks).
- **Catch-up.** The save's `saved_at` is where the catch-up starts; `tests/e2e/mock.ts` (`reopenAfter`) moves it back by editing the stored save, so a change to the save's key or that field breaks it. `tests/e2e/play.spec.ts` sets `stats.story.tension` and writes a whole `stats.rival` and `stats.friends` the same way, so it follows the shape of `Storyteller.toJSON`, `Rival.toJSON` and `Friends.toJSON`; it writes a whole `stats.pets` too, so it follows `Pets.toJSON`. `catchup.ts` copies the walker's goal odds (`goals.ts`), the trip rules (`trips.ts`, `game/journey.ts`) and when and where a flying mount flies (`legM`, copying `walker.ts` takeOff, all but its street-corner check, as there are no streets) instead of calling the walker, so a change to either belongs in both. Both call `Stats.feedDragon` before he moves (each step in `game.ts`, each leg in `catchup.ts`), so a dragon eats and sulks the same whether he was watched or not. Both call `Stats.collect()` after a real place's first-visit check and `Stats.arrive()` on a new city, so keepsakes and item stories match whether he was watched or not. Both call `Stats.happen()` at each stop, on the card dice, so incidents, club nights and meetings with Viktor and friends come the same way watched or not and never change his walk. A stray card (`stray` in `incidents.json`) may give him a pet there, and a high-level pet may show its fairy self at a real castle or monument, both on their own dice seeded from the time and place (a made-up place never counts; catch-up's made-up kinds hold no castle or monument). His pet eats on the first minute of each local day, in `Stats.newDay`. `Stats.passTime` rolls friends' news and anniversaries on the first minute of each local day, so the catch-up's steps get them too. Club nights, work chance cards (after a paid shift in `Stats.do`), news and anniversaries roll their own dice seeded from the time, place or date, so they never shift the walk's dice. Both board through `Stats.board` (the fare and ride line from `Stats.ride`, where a rascal may skip a bus ticket, then the kit from `Stats.packForTrip`, which decides abroad from `Stats.where`) and land through `Stats.land` (jet lag from `Stats.landFlight`; night train, cycling, a hitched lift or a long walk from `Stats.landRide`, by the plan's road distance, which the walker keeps as `Travel.road_m`) after letting the ride's time pass (`Stats.passTime`), so a ride's buff covers it; `Game.catchUp` leaves a ride still going at the end of the time away on its way instead of relocating him, write "Heading to…" through `trips.ts` `heading`, and weigh each place by `exploreWeight` (a whisper to explore; the walker takes it as `placePull`, apart from its per-kind `need`). It also opens a waiting care package at his first stop, like `game.ts` does at each arrival. Its made-up place kinds (`MADE_UP_KINDS`) must keep their own entries in `actions.json` (`tests/unit/catchup.test.ts` checks). A reopen's dilemma (`dilemmas.ts`, loaded only then) is rolled in `game.ts` after the catch-up and isn't saved: he decides it himself when the away card closes or the page is left.
- **Sheet heights.** `src/ui/snap.ts` `TALL` is the tallest a dragged sheet gets; `src/ui/app.css` `.sheet` `max-height: 60%` must match it.
- **His look.** `src/core/look.ts` names each pose's motion (`rise`, `pop`); `src/ui/app.css` has a `.bubble` class and `@keyframes` of the same name for each. Its place icons must cover every kind in `actions.json` (`tests/unit/look.test.ts` checks).
- **Bricks and life.** `Stats.bricksAt` runs after each "Reached" line in both `game.ts` (`onArrived`) and `catchup.ts`, with its own dice seeded from the time and place, so walk dice stay the same. `Stats.meetAt` (place creatures) runs after it in both (in `game.ts` after `collect`), with its own dice the same way. A creature's `at` in `data/creatures.json` names place classes (`bridge` from `ofm.ts` `BRIDGE`, `old_well` from `poiGen.ts` `ODD_KINDS`, the rest from the map); `tests/unit/creatures.test.ts` checks each still exists. A club kind's `at` in `src/core/clubs.ts` names place classes the same way (`wizard_tower` from `poiGen.ts` `TOWER_KIND`, the rest from the map); `tests/unit/clubs.test.ts` checks each still exists. Bridges are places made from the street layer in `ofm.ts` (`bridges`), not POIs, so `tools/make_placedex.py` adds their kind by hand (`FROM_STREETS`); a change to what a cell holds bumps `CACHE_VERSION` in `streets/source.ts`. A monument's map icon is `plaque` in `map/icons.ts` (`core/mapIcons.ts` `monumentIcons`, checked in `tests/unit/bricks.test.ts`), and `map/scene.ts` puts the number built in the icon key so a new one shows at once. `Stats.magicAt` (a ley burst, a spell from a book) runs right after `meetAt` in both, its own dice the same way, and `Stats.wordAt` (a local word, `core/words.ts`) right after it; the trickle along a line only happens in `game.ts` (`checkLey`, once a second), where the lines are worked out from the loaded places, and `map/scene.ts` draws `Game.leys` while `GameScreen` has the Stats sheet open. `Stats.giantAt` (giants seen from afar) runs right after `wordAt` in both (in `catchup.ts` at real places only), its own dice the same way; a giant's `at` in `core/giants.ts` names place classes or class/subclass kinds from `actions.json`, which `tests/unit/giants.test.ts` checks. `Stats.fairyAt` runs right after `giantAt` in both, on its own dice too, and takes the local hour (`Game.hour()`, `deps.hourAt` in the catch-up); a nap ends the stop like a quest step: `game.ts` rests him (capped at a minute, like every watched stop), `catchup.ts` lets its minutes pass. `Stats.portalAt` runs right after it in both, also on its own dice; an open portal ends the stop: `game.ts` calls `Walker.rideNow(city, "portal")` and returns, and `catchup.ts` lands him through `landIn`, the same as the end of a trip. The ley line colour in `map/mapView.ts` (`LEY_COLOR`) copies `--magic` in `ui/app.css` and `MAGIC` in `map/icons.ts`, as MapLibre can't read CSS tokens and the icons load lazily. The chronicle's milestones are written where they happen in `stats.ts` (`milestone()`); a save without one gets the old firsts marked done in `Stats.fromJSON`.
- **Made-up places.** `core/poiGen.ts` (a lazy chunk) makes them once per city in `game.ts` (`makePlaces`, once the area is loaded) and the save keeps them by city (`made_places`). They live in `StreetGraph.made`, which `placesIn` returns once their cell is loaded, so the walker, catch-up and city check treat them like real places. Every kind it makes needs an `actions.json` entry and a `look.ts` place icon, and every `ICON_OF` name a picture in `map/icons.ts` (`tests/unit/mapIcons.test.ts`). `Trips.nearestAirport` falls back to `madeAirport` (in `trips.ts`), so made airports work at both ends of a flight. Quests and events add icons through `MapPort.setMapIcons` with their own `kind`, and taps come back through `MapView.onIconTap`.
- **Postcards.** A postcard travels in the link's `#postcard=` fragment, so the format in `core/postcard.ts` must keep reading links already sent; `App.tsx` reads it on open and clears it from the address.
- **City cards.** `CARD_SHOPS` in `src/core/cityCards.ts` names the map kinds whose `actions.json` entry has the "pack": true action, and where he sells spares; `tests/unit/cityCards.test.ts` checks they match. The pool is built from `cities.tsv`, so rebuilding that file changes the cards (a saved card whose city left the list just stops counting).
- **Keepsakes in Help.** `src/core/collection.ts` words each crawl's place kinds by crawl id (`CRAWL_KINDS`) for Help and the Collection, so a crawl added to or changed in `keepsakes.ts` needs its words there. `tests/unit/help.test.ts` checks it. `NAP_BELOW` in `src/ui/help.ts` reads the energy limit of the park's "Nap under a tree" action in `actions.json` by name, so renaming or moving that action needs a change there; the same test checks it is filled.
- **The Collection registry.** `src/core/collection.ts` lists every set (`SETS`) and feat (`FEATS`) the Collection sheet, its Completion % and Help's Badges page show. A feature that adds something to collect (a mount, a quest type, a place kind, an item) adds its set or feat there in the same PR. Items count by base and material, read from the item's name (`partsOf`): `ownedIds` adds what is in his gear and bag now to `keepsakes.owned`, and `award` notes them there at each save. Made-up odd places are noted in `Stats.collect`, which knows extras by `extra` and a wizard's tower by its kind (`poiGen.TOWER_KIND`). `SOLD_AT` there words where each item kind (`KINDS` in `items.ts`) is sold, so a new kind needs its words; the type checks it.
- **Sky.** `stats.sky` is set by the game once a second and by the catch-up per step (with no weather: the user chose season only while away); it is never saved. Readers that no import shows: `skyNeed` in the walker and catch-up picks, and `openNow` in `quests.ts`. The weather forecast lives apart under `WEATHER_KEY` (`saveFile.ts`). `Game.sky()` sets `holiday` from the country's holiday list (`core/holidays.ts`), so holidays close day businesses through `opening.ts` and add the `_holiday` actions of `actions.json` (read by name in `activities.ts`, and named on Help's Public holidays page), watched or not. Actions' `needs.season` / `needs.weather` values must be in `SEASONS` / `WEATHER_NEEDS` (`tests/unit/sky.test.ts` checks). `tests/e2e/mock.ts` answers Open-Meteo with rain at 9°C. The place kinds in `opening.ts` (`DAY_ONLY`, `ALL_NIGHT`) and `sky.ts` (`OUTDOOR`, `SHELTER`, `COOLING`) are `actions.json` keys (class or class/subclass from `ofm.ts`); renaming a kind means changing them too (`tests/unit/sky.test.ts` checks they exist). `sky.ts` `skyNeed` wraps each place's need wherever needs feed `urgentRules` (`game.ts` `w.need` and catch-up's `pickPlace`): a closed place gets no pull and stops counting as urgent, and when every place near is closed both fall back to a random spot.
- **Needs.** The urgent and rock-bottom rules (`goals.ts` `urgentRules`) run in three places: the walker's goal pick, catch-up's `pickPlace` and `choose()` in `activities.ts`. While any meter is urgent, `game/journey.ts` and catch-up both skip the trip dice (`anyUrgent`).
- **Street cache.** `streets/idb.ts` prunes through an IndexedDB index on `Cached.t` (from `streets/source.ts`), so renaming that field or changing the store means a new database version with an upgrade step; a tab still running the older version blocks the upgrade; the new one then fetches streets from the network and closes its own connection when a newer version asks. `tests/unit/idb.test.ts` upgrades a version 1 database and covers both cases.
- **Tile decoding.** Only the streets worker decodes street tiles (`core/ofm.ts`, with `pbf` and `@mapbox/vector-tile`): `streets/source.ts` calls `fromPbf` on the bytes from `Api.streetTileBytes()`. Page code (anything `src/ui/` imports, `net/api.ts` included) must not import the decoder, or it lands in startup JS; `tests/unit/api.test.ts` reads `net/api.ts` and fails if it calls `fromPbf`.
- **Timed moments.** `public/data/moments.json` is keyed like `actions.json` (`class/subclass`, `class`, `_generic`, `_spot`), plus `_trip:` keys for moments on the way; `tests/unit/placeMoments.test.ts` fails on a kind of place with actions but no moment. A moment's pick rolls its own dice (`placeMoments.ts` `momentDice`), so `game.ts` (`askPlaceMoment`, when nobody taps) and `catchup.ts` (`Stats.byHimself`) land on the same outcome and his walk dice stay the same. Other features ask through `game.ask(moment)`; the catch-up never asks, it takes `defaultPick`.
- **Lazy chunks.** `GameScreen.tsx` imports `StatsSheet.tsx`, `CollectionSheet.tsx`, `AwaySheet.tsx`, `MomentSheet.tsx` and `CareSheet.tsx` (which reads the bond through `Bond` getters, so it pulls no extra startup chunk), `HelpSheet.tsx` imports `help.ts`, and `game.ts` imports `core/dilemmas.ts`, `core/iso2.ts` (the holiday country codes), `core/poiGen.ts` and `core/collection.ts` (once he is awake, to hand out badges at saves), `mapView.ts` imports `icons.ts`, with `import()` to keep them out of startup JS. The service worker only caches what the page fetches, so `src/main.tsx` fetches the same chunks at idle once it is ready; a new lazy `import()` that must work offline needs a line there too.
- **Perf budgets.** `tests/perf/budgets.json` sets the CI limits (transfer, first paint, ready, main thread, long tasks), checked against the median of three cold loads (`PERF_RUNS` changes the count). Startup JS size is not measured, but prefer choices that keep it small; a new dependency still shows in transfer.
- **Place classes in data.** A forecast's `effect` in `public/data/forecasts.json` is one of `EFFECTS` in `core/forecast.ts`, and its `kind` and the keys of `public/data/jokes.json` are place classes from `actions.json` (`tests/unit/forecast.test.ts` and `life.test.ts` check). Help lists the resale shops from the `resale` flags in `actions.json` (`{RESALE_SHOPS}`). Likewise the shops that open after midnight come from the `midnight` flags (`{MIDNIGHT_SHOPS}`), which `Stats.do` sends to the shop after midnight (`core/midnight.ts`). An action's `deed` (+1 kind, -1 rascal) in `actions.json` moves his lean and is listed in Help (`{KIND_DEEDS}`, `{RASCAL_DEEDS}`), and `letters.json` needs a list per `LETTER_AT` step and a thank-you per gift id (`tests/unit/bond.test.ts` checks).
- **Layout.** `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` opens every screen at 360 and 412 px and fails on overlap or sideways scroll. A new screen or sheet needs a step there. CI keeps the shots as the `screens` artifact.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/`: a file per module with logic worth testing alone (`walker.test.ts`, `help.test.ts`...); small or UI-only modules are covered through integration and e2e.
- `tests/integration/`: `game.test.ts` (game + walker + streets on fixture tiles) and `scene.test.ts` (scene + a fake map).
- `tests/e2e/`: Playwright on a Pixel 7 profile with mocked services (`mock.ts`). `live.spec.ts` plays the deployed site after each deploy.
